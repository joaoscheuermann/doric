# Feature: the workspace window

- Status: `implemented`

## Problem

The reader works in one window: they pick a Project, follow its Threads, read and steer a conversation, open child Threads, files and terminals beside it, and reach Settings and the state of the connection. The window has to hold all of it at once without losing a behavior when a bundle is missing, and it has to match the Doric-UI Figma design: a three-column window with no window-wide footer, borderless content on inset cards, compact pill tabs, and an account avatar in place of a status bar.

Three bundles draw it. [`layout`](../../bundles/desktop/layout/README.md) is the shell: the frame, its columns and the slots other bundles fill. [`workspace`](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/README.md) chooses what the window shows: the sidebar of Projects and Threads, the Project tabs and the Dashboard. [`account`](../../bundles/desktop/account/README.md) draws the reader's account status. This document says how the window behaves across them; each README holds the full shape of its bundle's contracts and slots, and [the layout architecture document](../architecture/bundles/layout.md) describes the shell's mechanisms.

## Decisions

- The Figma file's prototype page is the shell's source of truth: where a component page of the file disagrees with it, the prototype wins.
- The Files column (the Project panel) is always shown. It is 328 px wide by default, resizes between 14rem and 30rem, and cannot be hidden or collapsed. The account status sits in its header, and the right end of the Project tab bar holds the `shell.header.actions` items. The panel contract has one method, `select`.
- When zoom leaves the window narrower than the sum of the columns' minimums, the columns are drawn below their minimums; no column collapses to make room.
- While the selected panel view is not registered (its bundle absent or quarantined), the panel shows no view; it does not fall back to another view.
- The window has no window-wide footer; the account status holds what a status bar would: the avatar opens the account menu, which holds Settings, and the connection badge over the avatar's corner is the Figma cloud glyph (cloud when connected, cloud-off when disconnected, in the paper green and red, with the warning tone when a resource reading crosses its threshold). The badge is its own keyboard-reachable control, separate from the avatar, and the `resources` bundle builds its accessible description.
- The account menu shows `READER_NAME` and the version the app reports.
- Bundles adapt to the app they run on: a bundle that finds a platform contract or a `desktop/ui` module missing hides or disables the feature that needs it instead of failing. The account menu omits the version without `doric.about/v1`, and the sidebar header draws no Back and Forward without the history methods.
- A Project's menu has no "Copy path": every Project's sandbox root is `/workspace`.
- New terminal stays at the right end of the Project tab bar.
- The sidebar has no Threads pane or header. An icon-only New Thread button sits under the Thread tree, disabled while no Project is selected.
- The panes other bundles register (Screens, MCP) keep their header and collapse control, have no dividers and no resizing, and are pinned to the column's bottom; an open pane takes its content's height up to 40 % of the column and scrolls past that.
- Thread rows draw no repository mark.
- Every tree that shares the desktop tree guides indents 20 px per level.
- An Untitled Thread shows the Untitled icon instead of its kind icon on its row and its tabs.
- A Thread row shows the elapsed badge and the shimmer while the Thread or any descendant, at any depth, works: a running descendant, or a busy agent terminal under the Thread or a descendant.
- Expandable rows keep their lead icon on hover and focus; the trees draw no chevron, and Thread rows with children carry `aria-expanded`.
- Tree rows (Thread, subthread, terminal, draft, Files and Changes) and pane rows have hover, pressed, selected and keyboard-focus states: hover and pressed use Doric's paper ghost tokens, the selected fill is the sidebar accent and the focus outline is `--sidebar-ring`; the fill belongs to the whole row. Row actions and status reveals react to the pointer and to keyboard focus only.

## Current behavior and contracts

### The frame

- `<layout-workspace>`, registered at `/`, draws one of two forms ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx)):
  - the **page form**, while `shell.page` holds an item: a 280 px sidebar column (`shell.sidebar`, the quarantine notice, `shell.sidebar.footer`) beside the title bar (`shell.titlebar.tabs`) over the page (`shell.page`);
  - the **conversation form** otherwise: the sidebar column with the stack panes, the title bar over the conversation, the viewer column while a viewer tab is open, and the Files column.
- Both forms draw the same `shell.sidebar` region element, moved between them, so the sidebar's items do not remount when the form changes ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx)).
- In the conversation form the columns are resizable panels ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/templates/workspace-layout.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/templates/workspace-layout.tsx)):
  - the sidebar: 280 px by default, between 11rem and 24rem, never collapsed. One full-height, invisible resize handle owns its boundary; the handle draws no grip and the sidebar draws no divider;
  - the conversation: at least 16rem, on an inset card under the Project tab bar, with no breadcrumb or title header;
  - the viewer column: present only while a viewer tab is open, 448 px by default, between 18rem and 60rem, behind an invisible 8 px handle; its 40 px header holds the viewer tabs above an inset card;
  - the Files column: always present, 328 px by default, between 14rem and 30rem. Its 40 px header holds the Project panel's view toggles and, at its end, the `shell.window.actions` region; its footer is one 28 px row holding `shell.panel.footer`.
- The headers are window drag regions; their controls are not. On macOS the sidebar header keeps the `--doric-safe-left` padding for the window controls, which the window's chrome sets from its safe area and sets to 0 in fullscreen, where macOS hides the controls ([app/desktop/doric/src/platform/windows/appearance.ts](../../app/desktop/doric/src/platform/windows/appearance.ts), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/sidebar-header.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/sidebar-header.tsx), [packages/desktop/src/client/chrome.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/client/chrome.ts)). How the window draws its frame and theme is in [window appearance](../architecture/bundles/window-appearance.md).
- Every footer of the app, with its nested text and controls, uses font weight 400, set by one rule of the shell's stylesheet over `footer` and every `[data-slot$='-footer']` ([app/desktop/doric-renderer/src/styles.css](../../app/desktop/doric-renderer/src/styles.css)).
- When the window's bundle catalog lists quarantined bundles, one warning notice at the bottom of the sidebar names them ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/molecules/bundle-health-notice.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/molecules/bundle-health-notice.tsx); see [error messages](error-messages.md)).

### The Project tab bar

- `<workspace-project-tabs>` draws the 40 px Project tab bar in the title bar of both forms ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/elements/navigation-elements.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/elements/navigation-elements.tsx), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-tabs.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-tabs.tsx)). It is the workspace's only top header. It holds one tab per open Project, a "New project" button, and at its right end the `shell.window.actions` region on `/` and `/projects` and the `shell.header.actions` region elsewhere. It draws no Dashboard tab.
- A Project tab is a compact pill, 172 px wide and shrinking to 100 px before the strip scrolls, with the Project's colored avatar beside its name; the selected tab is the one whose Project the window path names. Its close control is subdued until hover; a middle click also closes the tab. A double click or the menu's Rename edits the name inline, which the host renames. The tab's context menu has New thread, Rename and the Project's color, then Close tab, Close other tabs, Close tabs to the right and Close all tabs; a close entry that would close nothing is hidden ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-tabs.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-tabs.tsx)).
- The strip scrolls horizontally with the trackpad or the mouse wheel, hides its scrollbar, and reveals the selected tab after a selection, a reorder or a resize ([packages/desktop/src/panels/use-tab-strip.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/panels/use-tab-strip.ts)).
- The open tabs, their order, each tab's last path and the last Dashboard path are saved in the window's local storage under `doric.project-tabs`. Every route change remembers its path for its Project's tab, so navigating to a Project with no tab opens one, and tabs whose Projects do not exist are dropped once the Projects load. Closing the active tab moves to its neighbor, and closing the last one returns to `/projects` ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-project-tabs.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-project-tabs.ts), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/project-tabs.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/project-tabs.ts), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/stores/project-tabs.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/stores/project-tabs.ts)). The route subscription is made once per mount and disposed on unmount.
- "New project" opens `/` in its home form: a centered column with the "New project" button (⌘N or Ctrl+N), "Browse all projects", a search field and the ten most recent Projects, with "See all projects" under them. It does not open the create form by itself. The home form is a transient mark of the window: at startup, on reload and after any other navigation, `/` shows the full Project list ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/stores/new-tab.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/stores/new-tab.ts), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard.tsx)).

### Tab sorting

Every tab bar of the window (the Project tabs, the viewer tabs and the Project panel's view toggles) sorts its tabs the same way, each bar in its own sorting context ([packages/desktop/src/panels/use-tab-strip.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/panels/use-tab-strip.ts), [packages/desktop/src/panels/tab-strip.tsx](../../packages/desktop/src/panels/tab-strip.tsx)):

- dnd-kit with a 6 px pointer activation threshold, horizontal movement only, animated insertion previews and automatic scrolling at the bar's edges; tab and close-button widths stay fixed during a drag; dropping commits the order and Escape cancels it;
- Space starts keyboard sorting, the arrows move and Space or Enter confirms; Alt+Shift+Left and Alt+Shift+Right move the focused tab by one place.

### The Dashboard

- On `/` and `/projects` the frame takes its page form: `workspace` draws the Dashboard's sidebar in `shell.sidebar`, its content in `shell.page` and its footer entries in `shell.sidebar.footer` ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/elements/navigation-elements.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/elements/navigation-elements.tsx)). The title bar's end holds the account status.
- The Dashboard's sidebar holds its header (Dashboard, Search and Back and Forward), "Search projects", "All projects", a Projects group, the `workspace.dashboard.nav` groups other bundles add and, at its foot, the `workspace.dashboard.footer` entries, among them `settings`' "All settings". It has no Home link and no brand heading. ⌘F or Ctrl+F focuses its search ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard.tsx), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard-sidebar.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard-sidebar.tsx)).
- `/projects` is a list on an inset card: the "Projects" heading with "New project", 64 px from the card's top, then a table of Name, Activity, Created and State that starts 60 px below the heading (the heading's 12 px bottom padding and the list's 48 px top padding). A row places the Project's colored avatar beside its name, with no thumbnail. The list has no view selector ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard.tsx), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/project-item.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/project-item.tsx)).
- A Project row's context menu has Open project, the Project's color (set or clear) and Delete project; the row has no ellipsis button. Keyboard focus on a Project row draws a 2 px `--paper-focus` outline ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/project-item.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/project-item.tsx)).
- Projects keep the color the host assigns them from a fixed palette, shown by their avatar ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/project-avatar.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/project-avatar.tsx)). Navigation between Projects and their actions live in the Project tabs and the Dashboard.
- Another bundle can show a page in the card instead of the Project list through `workspace.dashboard/v1`: `active()` answers the shown `workspace.dashboard.page` item's id, `show(id)` shows one (an id no item has is kept and draws the list; an empty id is refused), `close()` returns to the list and `subscribe` follows changes. At most one page shows at a time, and "All projects" closes it ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/contracts/navigation.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/contracts/navigation.ts), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard.tsx)). [`settings`](../../bundles/desktop/settings/README.md) fills it with its provider page ([bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/index.ts)).
- Creating a Project also creates its first Thread, with an empty name, which the tree shows as Untitled until the reader names it, and opens it selected. When that Thread cannot be created, the Project, already saved, is opened and the failure is reported, without creating the Project again ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-workspace.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-workspace.ts)).

### The sidebar

On a Project route `<workspace-sidebar>` draws the Project sidebar, and the layout adds the stack panes under it ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx), [bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx)). From top to bottom:

- the 40 px header: the Dashboard button, which opens `/projects`, and Back and Forward ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/sidebar-header.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/sidebar-header.tsx));
- "Search Threads", which ⌘F or Ctrl+F focuses from anywhere but an editor or a terminal; Escape clears it and moves the focus to the tree;
- the icon-only Chat and Archived tabs, which split the tree (see [thread archive](thread-archive.md));
- the selected Project's recursive Thread tree, with no Threads label, then, on the Chat tab only, the icon-only New Thread button, drawn with `--paper-ghost-hover` at rest and `--paper-ghost-pressed` under the pointer;
- the stack panes, pinned to the column's bottom.

The tree:

- The sidebar draws only the selected Project's Threads. Threads start expanded, and each keeps its own expansion while the sidebar is mounted; selecting a Thread never closes another branch ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx)).
- A row is 28 px high, with a 16 px lead slot, and indents 20 px per level (6 px plus 20 px per level) ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/thread-branches.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/thread-branches.tsx), [packages/desktop/src/panels/tree-guides.tsx](../../packages/desktop/src/panels/tree-guides.tsx)). The lead slot holds the Thread's kind icon, or `message-circle-dashed` for an Untitled Thread ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/thread-mark.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/thread-mark.tsx)); the chat's viewer tab for a Thread wears the same icon ([bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/thread-tab-icon.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/thread-tab-icon.tsx)). The name uses the regular font weight.
- A row is a `treeitem`; a row with children carries `aria-expanded`, and only the selected Thread's row has `aria-selected="true"`. The selected Thread is always a root Thread, since a child opens in a tab. Clicking the selected Thread's row, when it has children, opens or closes it; while a search is set it does neither ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/thread-branches.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/thread-branches.tsx)).
- Other bundles add rows under each Thread through `workspace.sidebar/v1` `addThreadRows({ id, tag, count, subscribe, busy? })`: the `tag` element is mounted with the `thread-id` attribute before the Thread's child Threads, a `count` above 0 makes the Thread row expandable, and the rows are not drawn in the Archived tab ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/contracts/navigation.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/contracts/navigation.ts), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/thread-branches.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/thread-branches.tsx)). [`terminal`](../../bundles/features/terminal/README.md) adds its terminal rows there.
- Fills ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/sidebar.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/sidebar.ts)): an unselected row fills its whole row with `--paper-ghost-hover` on hover and `--paper-ghost-pressed` while pressed; a selected row takes the sidebar accent with no hover or pressed fill; keyboard focus on the row, or on its name field while its name is being edited, draws an inset 2 px `--sidebar-ring` outline. The same states apply to the terminal rows under a Thread, whose row is selected while its tab is the selected tab of the Thread the route names ([bundles/features/terminal/README.md](../../bundles/features/terminal/README.md)), and to the Files and Changes trees ([files](files.md), [git](git.md)).
- The trailing slot shows the elapsed badge or a mark at rest; hover or keyboard focus, not a click alone, reveals the row's actions over it: the plus ("New sub-thread in …", on a main Thread only) and the archive actions. Row actions draw a `--paper-ghost-pressed` square under the pointer and a 2 px `--sidebar-ring` outline outside themselves on keyboard focus ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/row-add-action.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/row-add-action.tsx)).
- The row's context menu creates a child, opens the Thread in a tab, pins and archives it, deletes it where its kind allows, and copies its Thread ID ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/item-context-menu.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/item-context-menu.tsx)). A double click renames the Thread inline. A Thread's kind decides which of these it offers ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/thread-kind.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/thread-kind.ts); the workspace README lists the kinds). Delete asks for confirmation and terminates the Thread before deleting it ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-workspace.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-workspace.ts)).
- The row's hover title names its kind and, once the Thread has finished, the duration of its last execution ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/thread-branches.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/thread-branches.tsx)).

Activity:

- A Thread is active while it runs, while any descendant at any depth is active, or while a `busy(threadId)` registration of `workspace.sidebar/v1` answers true for it; the [`terminal`](../../bundles/features/terminal/README.md) bundle answers true for a Thread with a busy agent terminal. Every Thread of the Project counts, collapsed or filtered out ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/thread-tree.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/thread-tree.ts), `threadActivity`).
- An active row's name shimmers. Its trailing slot shows the elapsed time since the Thread's own last start until hover or keyboard focus reveals the row's actions: `mm:ss`, `h:mm:ss` up to 99 hours, `{d}d {h}h` up to 99 days, then `99d+` ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/utility/duration.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/utility/duration.ts)). The badge is a status named "Agent running". An active row with no start time shimmers with no badge and keeps a visually hidden "Agent running" status ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/row-add-action.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/row-add-action.tsx)).
- The start time is the Thread's `executionStartedAt` while it runs, and otherwise its last result's time less `lastExecutionMs` ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/thread-tree.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/thread-tree.ts)). The runtime sets `executionStartedAt` to the time of the running prompt's latest `prompt.started` event, else to the Thread's last update, and `lastExecutionMs` to the time from that event to the result of the same prompt ([bundles/runtime/runtime/src/host/store/threads.ts](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/src/host/store/threads.ts)).

Drafts:

- Creating a Project or a Thread starts as a focused local draft row: a submission with an empty name keeps the draft in place, and blur or Escape discards it without a call to the host ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/editable-name.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/editable-name.tsx)).
- The draft row takes the unselected row fill and the name field's outline, and wears the mark the entity will wear: the Project's color mark, empty until the host assigns a color, or the message icon of a Thread ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/draft-name.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/draft-name.tsx)).
- Beginning a draft clears the search and opens the Chat tab, so the draft row shows ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx)).

The stack panes (`layout.stack/v2`):

- A bundle registers a pane with its id, title, order, whether the reader can collapse it and whether it starts open; it declares no size. The layout draws one group per pane, in ascending order, under the Thread region ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/contracts/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/contracts/index.ts), [bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/domain/sidebar-stack.ts](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/domain/sidebar-stack.ts)).
- Each group has a 28 px header with its title and collapse control; the header's own action shows only while the header is hovered or holds focus. An open pane is as tall as its content, at most 40 % of the column, and scrolls past that; when the column is short the Thread region gives up its height first, and a pane never shrinks past its header ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx)).
- Today [`screen`](../../bundles/features/screen/README.md) registers Screens, which lists the current Project's views, and [`mcp`](../../bundles/features/mcp/README.md) registers the MCP servers; both start collapsed ([bundles/features/screen/src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/features/screen/src/client/index.ts), [bundles/features/mcp/src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/features/mcp/src/client/index.ts)). An empty open pane says `No screens open` (with a Project selected) or `No MCP servers` (once loaded, without a failure). Pane rows take the tree rows' fills and outline, and their actions show on row hover or keyboard focus only; see [screen](screen.md) and [MCP](mcp.md).

### Selection and live updates

- The selection is the window path: `/` (the Dashboard), `/projects` (the full Project list), `/projects/:projectId` (a Project) or `/projects/:projectId/threads/:threadId` (a Thread). The window keeps its path across restarts, and a restored selection is validated against the host ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-route-selection.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-route-selection.ts)). Other bundles read it as ids through `workspace.selection/v1`.
- Opening a Project without a Thread selects its first root Thread that is not archived once its Threads load ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/project-tree.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/project-tree.ts), `withSelection`). Returning to a Project tab opens its remembered path.
- `workspace` reads Projects and Threads from the window's `runtime.records/v1` mirror ([`runtime`](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/README.md)) and keeps the Threads it has read for each Project ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/stores/workspace.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/stores/workspace.ts), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/project-tree.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/project-tree.ts)). It follows only the selected Project's live updates, so a Thread an agent creates there appears without a refresh ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-workspace.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-workspace.ts)).

### Conversation and viewer tabs

- Selecting a root Thread shows its conversation in the conversation column ([`chat`](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md); see [conversation](conversation.md)).
- Selecting a child Thread, from the tree or from a link in a conversation, keeps the current root Thread of the same Project in the conversation column, or the child's own root when the current one is in another Project, and asks for the child beside it through `workspace.hooks/v1` `onOpenThreadHook`. A direct child route first establishes its root conversation and canonicalizes the route to it ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/thread-navigation.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/thread-navigation.ts), [bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/thread-navigation.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/domain/thread-navigation.ts)).
- The chat answers by opening a reusable viewer tab `thread:<id>` through `layout.tabs/v2`, titled with the Thread's name or "Untitled". When the Thread's name changes the tab is retitled; when the Thread leaves its Project's described Threads the tab is closed in every owner ([bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/thread-tabs.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/thread-tabs.ts)). Without the chat the hook opens nothing.
- The frame shows the tabs of the owner `workspace` names with `setOwner`: the route's Thread, else the Project ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/index.ts)). Switching tabs keeps each mounted view; closing a tab closes only the view and neither stops nor deletes the Thread ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx), [bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/organisms/workspace-tabs.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/organisms/workspace-tabs.tsx)).

### The Project panel

- The Files column's header draws the `shell.panel` views as one joined group of icon-only toggles, each with its accessible name and only the selected one pressed; they can be reordered, and the order is kept only while the frame is mounted, not saved ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/organisms/project-panel.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/organisms/project-panel.tsx), [packages/desktop/src/panels/tab-strip.tsx](../../packages/desktop/src/panels/tab-strip.tsx)). Today they are Files and Changes ([files](files.md), [git](git.md)).
- The panel starts with no view selected and draws the selected view only while it is registered; `layout.panel/v3` `select(id)` selects one, and an id no item has yet is kept until that item registers ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/services/layout-panel.ts](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/services/layout-panel.ts)). `files` selects the Files view when it activates.

### The account status

- `account` adds `<account-status>` to `shell.window.actions`: the end of the Files column's header in the conversation form, the end of the Project tab bar in the page form ([bundles/desktop/account/src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/account/src/client/index.ts)).
- It is the reader's avatar, a button named "Account" that opens the account menu, with the `account.status` slot in a 14×14 px box over the avatar's bottom-right corner; each item there is its own Tab stop ([bundles/desktop/account/src/client/components/organisms/account-status.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/account/src/client/components/organisms/account-status.tsx)). [`resources`](../../bundles/features/resources/README.md) puts its connection badge there: the cloud glyph in paper green when connected, the cloud-off glyph in paper red when disconnected, and the cloud in the warning color when a resource reading crosses its threshold; it opens the resource readings ([bundles/features/resources/src/client/monitor/connection-badge.tsx](../../bundles/features/resources/src/client/monitor/connection-badge.tsx)).
- The account menu shows `READER_NAME` above "Local account", then the `account.menu` entries, then the About line, "Doric" above "MIT License · v<version>" while `doric.about/v1` gives a version and "MIT License" otherwise ([bundles/desktop/account/src/client/domain/about.ts](../../bundles/desktop/account/src/client/domain/about.ts)). An `account.menu` item with malformed props is not drawn, and the bundle reports it once on the console ([bundles/desktop/account/src/client/hooks/use-menu-items.ts](../../bundles/desktop/account/src/client/hooks/use-menu-items.ts)). [`settings`](../../bundles/desktop/settings/README.md) adds the "Settings" entry, so Settings opens from the avatar in two clicks ([bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/index.ts)). Without `settings` the menu has no entry; without `resources` the corner is empty.
