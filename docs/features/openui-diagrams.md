# Feature: diagrams in OpenUI fences

- Status: `implemented`

## Problem

The agent answers with UI in an `openui-lang` fence of its answer ([generative UI](generative-ui.md)): forms, tables and charts. A process, an exchange of messages, a data model or a hierarchy is structure, not numbers, and drawn as text it is hard to read. So the agent needs diagrams it writes like any other component, laid out by the app and drawn in the app's theme, which the reader can pan and zoom.

## Decisions

- The diagram types are flowcharts, sequence diagrams, architecture (grouped regions with connections), ER or class diagrams, and mind maps or trees. There is no draw.io.
- The agent describes a diagram with native OpenUI components in OpenUI Lang: a diagram component and its parts (nodes, edges and the like). The app lays it out automatically and draws it with its own theme. The app draws no Mermaid.
- Diagrams exist only in `openui-lang` fences, not in papercraft fences.
- The reader can pan and zoom a diagram. Its parts take no click action.
- A diagram has no export.
- ELK is the layout engine, and its license is accepted. dagre and d3-hierarchy do not ship.
- A fence whose wrapper is `inert` shows a static, fitted diagram. The renderer alone decides when its wrapper is `inert`; diagrams add no rule to it.
- A sequence diagram has notes, fragments and their sections (`Note`, `Fragment`, `Section`).
- A class diagram's methods are plain strings.
- Nodes carry no icons.
- The limits are: 100 nodes, 200 edges, 25 groups and 4 levels per `Flowchart`; 20 participants and 200 steps per `SequenceDiagram`; 60 entities and 120 relations per `EntityDiagram`; 300 topics and 8 levels per `MindMap`; labels cut at 120 characters (code points).
- The area of every diagram type is square: as wide as its fence, as tall as it is wide, following the width on resize. A diagram in a row `Stack` takes its slot's width. The drawing starts fitted into the area, never above 100 %, and refits on resize until the reader pans or zooms.
- The area's height is capped so that its toolbar and the whole drawing are visible in the window: the area stays square while it fits, and in a short window it is wider than tall.
- No diagram component takes a `height`.
- While its fence streams, a diagram shows an outline of its parts; once the fence is closed or cut off, it is laid out once and drawn.
- A diagram's findings (parts cut at a limit, unknown references, misplaced parts) reach the reader's error card and the agent through the conversation's program check, one line per statement. A failed layout or a layout chunk that does not load is shown in the diagram and is not reported to the agent: the agent cannot fix it.
- Sequence diagrams are laid out by Doric's own code and never load ELK.
- The pan and zoom math is one set of pure helpers in `packages/desktop`, shared by the diagrams and papercraft's canvas.

## Current behavior and contracts

The feature lives in the `openui` bundle (version 2.3.4, [manifest.json](../../bundles/features/openui/manifest.json)); its [README](../../bundles/features/openui/README.md) gives the bundle's full shape. The [`papercraft`](../../bundles/features/papercraft/README.md) bundle has no diagrams; it shares only the viewport helpers below. No contract of `openui/contracts` carries a diagram type ([src/contracts/index.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/contracts/index.ts)): the components live in the client part.

### Components

- The conversation library adds four diagram components and their parts ([library.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/library.ts), [diagram/definitions.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/definitions.ts)). Only the diagram component draws; its parts draw nothing on their own, and the diagram reads them from its own props.
  - `Flowchart(nodes, edges?, groups?, direction?)`, `direction` `right`, otherwise `down`, with `Node(id, label, shape?, group?)` (`shape` `box` by default, `round`, `diamond`, `circle` or `cylinder`), `Edge(from, to, label?, line?, arrow?)` (`line` `solid` by default or `dashed`; `arrow` `end` by default or `none`) and `Group(id, label, parent?)`.
  - `SequenceDiagram(participants, steps?)` with `Participant(id, label)`, `Message(from, to, text, kind?)` (`kind` `call` by default, `reply` or `async`; `from` equal to `to` is a self-message), `Note(text, over)` with `over` a list of participant ids, `Fragment(kind, sections)` (`loop`, `opt`, `alt` or `par`) and `Section(label, steps)`. `steps` holds `Message`, `Note` and `Fragment`.
  - `EntityDiagram(entities, relations?, notation?, direction?)`, `notation` `er` by default or `class`, `direction` `right`, otherwise `down`, with `Entity(id, name, fields?, methods?)`, `Field(name, type?, key?)` (`key` `pk`, `fk`, `pk-fk` or `unique`) and `Relation(from, to, kind?, label?, fromCount?, toCount?)` (`kind` `association` by default, `inherits`, `implements`, `composition`, `aggregation` or `dependency`; counts `1`, `0..1`, `*` or `1..*`).
  - `MindMap(root, layout?)`, `layout` `right` by default (a mind map) or `down` (a tree), with the recursive `Topic(label, children?)`.
- The lists after the first (`edges`, `steps`, `relations`) are optional, so a diagram stays drawn while its later lists have not streamed yet. Recursion (`Section.steps`, `Topic.children`) is written with zod getters, so the skill prints `(Message | Note | Fragment)[]` and `Topic[]` ([diagram/definitions.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/definitions.ts)).

### Reading a diagram and its findings

- One pure reading per type turns the props into the drawn model and a list of findings in a fixed order ([diagram/model.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/model.ts), [diagram/sequence-model.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/sequence-model.ts), [diagram/entity-model.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/entity-model.ts), [diagram/mindmap-model.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/mindmap-model.ts), shared helpers in [diagram/read.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/read.ts)). Every departure is drawn the best way possible and, except where a bullet below says otherwise, named in a finding:
  - an id outside 1 to 64 characters, or repeated, is not drawn (only the first of a repeated id is);
  - parts beyond a count limit are not drawn (`The <Diagram> has <N> <parts>, and only the first <limit> are drawn: split the diagram.`);
  - a label, name, field, method or message text longer than 120 code points is cut to 120, ending in `…`;
  - an empty label or name of a `Node`, `Group`, `Entity` or `Participant` is drawn as the part's id, with no finding ([diagram/model.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/model.ts), [diagram/entity-model.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/entity-model.ts), [diagram/sequence-layout.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/sequence-layout.ts));
  - an edge, message, note or relation that names an unknown id is not drawn; a `Node` naming an unknown group is drawn outside any group; a `Group` naming an unknown parent, in a parent loop, or nested deeper than 4 levels is drawn at the top level; a `Group` with no node is not drawn;
  - a `Topic` deeper than 8 levels is not drawn, with its subtopics; a `Fragment` with no step is not drawn; a sequence part in the wrong list (for example a `Section` straight in `steps`) is not drawn;
  - a diagram with nothing to draw says so (`The Flowchart has no nodes.` and the like).
- Under `er`, every relation reads as `association` and methods are not read, both without a finding ([diagram/entity-model.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/entity-model.ts)).
- `diagramCheck` walks the parsed program through one table of the four types and their parts (`DIAGRAM_PARTS`). A part outside its diagram gives `<Part> draws only inside a <Diagram>.`; a diagram inside another diagram's props gives `<Inner> is not drawn inside a <Outer>.` and adds nothing from its contents. A statement's findings become one `runtime-error` whose message is `Diagram problems (<N>): 1. … 2. …`, at most 300 UTF-16 code units, ending with `<K> more not shown.` when some do not fit ([diagram/check.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/check.ts)).
- `mergeDiagramErrors` appends that message after `; ` to the first `runtime-error` of the same statement when there is one, refitted to 300 UTF-16 code units, and otherwise adds it as its own line. Both `programCheck`, whose lines the error card under the fence shows, and `check`, which the error reporter sends to the agent, merge the diagram findings ([check.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/check.ts)). How errors reach the agent is described in [generative UI](generative-ui.md).

### Streaming, layout and the layout chunk

- Each diagram view shows a text outline of its parts with a note while it cannot draw: outside a conversation fence (`Diagrams are drawn only in the conversation.`), while its fence streams (`The diagram is drawn when its fence is complete.`) and while its layout is pending (`Drawing the diagram…`). Once the fence is `closed` or `unterminated`, the view lays the diagram out once per mount and drawn model, drops results that arrive late, and draws the picture with the outline kept for screen readers ([diagram/flowchart.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/flowchart.tsx), [diagram/sequence.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/sequence.tsx), [diagram/entity.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/entity.tsx), [diagram/mindmap.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/mindmap.tsx), [diagram/view.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/view.ts), [diagram/outline.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/outline.tsx)).
- A failed layout, or a layout chunk that did not load, shows the outline with `This diagram could not be drawn: ` and the first 200 characters of the error; nothing is reported to the agent ([diagram/view.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/view.ts)).
- Labels are measured in the drawing's font once the faces their characters need have loaded, and wrapped; boxes are sized to fit them ([diagram/measure.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/measure.ts)).
- `Flowchart`, `EntityDiagram` and `MindMap` are laid out by ELK in the layout chunk `client/chunks/layout.js`, built from [chunks/layout.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/chunks/layout.ts). The renderer chunk loads it through one layout loader per window, at the first diagram ELK lays out; a rejected load rejects that layout and every later one ([chunks/renderer.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/chunks/renderer.ts), [diagram/layout-loader.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/layout-loader.ts)). The renderer and the layout chunk share only types ([diagram/layout-types.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/layout-types.ts)): the input is a graph of keyed sizes, with no label, id or color.
- The chunk runs `layout(graph)` ([chunks/layout.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/chunks/layout.ts)):
  - flowcharts and entity diagrams with ELK `layered`, hierarchy handling for groups and orthogonal routing; mind maps with ELK `mrtree` ([diagram/graph.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/graph.ts));
  - an entity diagram's graph adds spacing for line-end markers. Under `class`, its counts are ELK end labels, and an `inherits` or `implements` relation is laid out from `to` (the parent) to `from` (the child), so parents sit above (`down`) or left of (`right`) their children; the drawing reverses its points. Under `er`, counts are drawn as markers and are not in the graph ([diagram/graph.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/graph.ts));
  - a `class` diagram laid out `right` is laid out `down` with every width and height swapped and transposed back, while an `er` diagram uses ELK's `RIGHT`;
  - one ELK instance per window, seeded, so the same graph gives the same result;
  - a graph ELK cannot lay out is refused before ELK runs with `Invalid layout graph: <problem>`, and an ELK failure rejects with ELK's error.
- The chunk contains `elkjs` 0.11.1, pinned, under the Eclipse Public License 2.0, with ELK's notices and where its source is available in the file's header ([chunks/layout.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/chunks/layout.ts), [package.json](../../bundles/features/openui/package.json)).
- `SequenceDiagram` is laid out by Doric's `layoutSequence` in the renderer chunk: columns in participant order, rows in step order, fragments as boxes around their rows. It never reads the layout loader, so a sequence never loads the layout chunk ([diagram/sequence-layout.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/sequence-layout.ts), [diagram/sequence.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/sequence.tsx)).
- `ConversationFence` takes an optional `layout` prop, used by tests and stories in place of the window's loader, and passes the layout and `interactive` to the diagrams through the fence context ([chunks/renderer.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/chunks/renderer.ts), [question/fence-context.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/question/fence-context.ts)).

### Drawing

- Each diagram is one SVG picture drawn by React (`svg[role="img"]`, labelled with the outline's header and described by the outline). Labels are text nodes, never markup ([diagram/canvas.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/canvas.tsx), [diagram/sequence-picture.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/sequence-picture.tsx), [diagram/entity-picture.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/entity-picture.tsx), [diagram/mindmap-picture.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/mindmap-picture.tsx)).
- Every color is a role over Doric's tokens, or `--foreground` mixed over `--background`, so a theme switch repaints without a new layout ([diagram/colors.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/colors.ts)).
- In a sequence diagram, a `call` message is a solid line with a filled arrowhead, a `reply` a dashed line with an open arrowhead, and an `async` a solid line with an open arrowhead ([diagram/sequence-picture.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/sequence-picture.tsx)).
- In an entity diagram, under `er` each end with a count carries its marker (`1`, `0..1`, `*`, `1..*`); under `class` counts are text at the line ends, `inherits` and `implements` draw a hollow triangle at `to`, `composition` a filled diamond and `aggregation` a hollow one at `from`, and `dependency` an open arrow at `to`; `implements` and `dependency` are dashed. So a child points to its parent, and a whole to its part ([diagram/entity-picture.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/entity-picture.tsx)).

### The area, pan and zoom

- `DiagramFrame` draws every type's area: as wide as its container and as tall as it is wide (`aspect-square w-full`), with its height capped at `calc(100svh - 40px - var(--doric-titlebar-height,40px) - 32px)` ([diagram/canvas.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/canvas.tsx)).
- `useDiagramViewport` fits the drawing into the area, never above 100 % and never below 25 %, and refits whenever the area resizes until the reader pans or zooms. The scale stays within 25–400 % ([diagram/gestures.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/gestures.ts)).
- The math is the pure helpers of `desktop/ui/viewport` (`fitViewport`, `zoomAt`, `clampScale`, `wheelGesture` and the rest) ([packages/desktop/src/viewport.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/viewport.ts)). Papercraft's canvas uses the same helpers: its viewport module re-exports the viewport math, and its gestures import `wheelGesture` directly ([bundles/features/papercraft/src/client/surface/viewport.ts](../../bundles/features/papercraft/src/client/surface/viewport.ts), [bundles/features/papercraft/src/client/surface/gestures.ts](../../bundles/features/papercraft/src/client/surface/gestures.ts)).
- A diagram is interactive exactly when `blockedReason` gives no reason, that is when the fence is in a main Thread (not an agent's), `closed`, and the latest revision of its program ([chunks/renderer.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/chunks/renderer.ts)). An interactive area is focusable and labelled `Flowchart, pan and zoom`, `Sequence diagram, pan and zoom`, `ER diagram, pan and zoom` or `Class diagram, pan and zoom`, or `Mind map, pan and zoom` or `Tree, pan and zoom`, with a toolbar (`Zoom out`, the zoom percentage, `Zoom in`, `Fit to view`). A pointer press focuses it, `Escape` blurs it, a drag pans, a wheel with Ctrl or Cmd zooms at the pointer, and a plain wheel pans only while the area has focus and otherwise scrolls the page ([diagram/canvas.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/canvas.tsx), [diagram/sequence.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/sequence.tsx), [diagram/entity.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/entity.tsx), [diagram/mindmap.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/mindmap.tsx), [diagram/gestures.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/gestures.ts)).
- Otherwise the diagram is static: no toolbar, no focus, no listeners, and the viewport is the fit; a diagram that stops being interactive returns to the fit ([diagram/canvas.tsx](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/canvas.tsx), [diagram/gestures.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/diagram/gestures.ts)).
- While a click of the same fence is sending, the fence's wrapper is `inert`, so input does not reach the area, but the diagram stays interactive: its toolbar stays drawn and its viewport keeps its pan and zoom ([chunks/renderer.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/chunks/renderer.ts)).

### The skill

- The `openui` skill lists the sixteen components' signatures (four diagrams and twelve parts) and five diagram rules ([src/host/skills/openui.md](../../bundles/features/openui/src/host/skills/openui.md)). The file is generated by [tests/client/skill-text.test.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/tests/client/skill-text.test.ts), which compares it with the generated text and writes it when run with `OPENUI_SKILL_UPDATE=1`. The rules:
  - draw structure with a diagram and numbers with a chart, choosing the type by what is drawn, and put a diagram at the root or in a `Stack`;
  - write a diagram right after `root`, then its lists in a fixed order, each on one line (a flowchart's `nodes`, `groups`, `edges`; a mind map's first-level branches one statement each), and patch only the statements that change, each written whole;
  - ids have 1 to 64 characters and are unique among one diagram's parts of a type; references name ids of the same diagram; never give positions, sizes or colors;
  - the limits per diagram, and split anything larger;
  - in a class diagram methods are plain strings, `inherits` and `implements` point from the child to the parent, `composition` and `aggregation` from the whole to the part.
