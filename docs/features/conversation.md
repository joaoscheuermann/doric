# Feature: Conversation

- Status: `implemented`

## Problem

The reader works with an agent through one Thread at a time. They need to read the agent's answers, reasoning and tool calls as they stream in, write the next prompt in the same place, and steer, queue, pause or stop the work while it runs. A long transcript needs a way to find a past prompt and to come back to the end. The controls that act on the Thread must stay in reach without cutting the transcript short, and other bundles must be able to draw their own events, tools and fences inside the conversation without the conversation knowing them.

## Decisions

- The conversation footer floats over the bottom of its conversation, and the transcript scrolls on behind it. Both footers, the conversation card's and a child Thread's footer in the viewer card, carry one class set for the band's geometry: `absolute inset-x-0 bottom-0 flex items-center justify-end gap-1 py-2 pl-2 text-[11px] font-normal`. The band is 44 px: 8 px over and under its 28 px row. Each owner's own sheet keys the band's height (`var(--shell-conversation-footer-height)`), its fill (`--background` at 70 %) and its blur (2 px) on the footer's `data-slot`, and each owner sets `--shell-conversation-footer-height: 2.75rem` beside its band.
- The shell sets `--shell-conversation-footer-height` on its conversation frame, `<chat-thread-panel>` sets it beside its own band, and the chat reads it. The chat's sheet ends the conversation's vertical scrollbar at `calc(100% - var(--shell-conversation-footer-height, 0px))` and gives the scroll viewport `scroll-padding-bottom: var(--shell-conversation-footer-height, 0px)`, so a block the browser scrolls into view stops above the band. Unset, the variable counts as 0 and the bar is full height.
- The band takes the pointer: no pointer rule is added, so a click, a wheel or a touch on the band goes to the band or its items, and a wheel over the band does not scroll the conversation.
- The chat's sheet isolates the scroll area (`isolation: isolate`); the band, positioned and later in tree order, paints over it with no `z-index`.
- The transcript column keeps its `pb-80`, so the end of the conversation still scrolls clear of the band.
- The conversation card draws no bottom padding (`pb-0`), so the conversation and its band reach the card's bottom edge.
- The viewer card draws no bottom padding while its selected tab asks for an open bottom (`openBottom` on a `layout.tabs/v2` tab), and keeps its padding for every other tab.
- The caret of a prompt being typed can stop under the floating band; this is accepted as a known limitation.
- The footer bands define no colour: their items draw in `--foreground` and only the separators are muted. The execution picker's root has `mr-auto` and anchors the left of the band.
- The chat's composer is the item `{ id: 'composer', tag: 'chat-composer', order: 40 }` of the slot `shell.conversation.footer`.
- The Conversation Trail sits in its own 26 px column left of the conversation's scroll area — 8 px padding, a 10 px bar box and 8 px padding — not inside the content's padding, so the scrollbar stays at the pane's right edge. Its bars are left-aligned in their box and grow rightwards to 17 px on hover, and its popover opens to the right of the handle. The conversation column is `pl-[1.875rem] pr-4 pt-0.5`, and below the column's own width (`@container (max-width: 48rem)`) the column's padding widens to `padding-inline-start: 2.5rem; padding-top: 1rem`.
- The chat computes each fence's key `<promptId>/<turnSequence>/<index>` and its state, `streaming`, `closed` or `unterminated`. A queue receipt ends no turn: an answer still streaming continues in the same turn, with the receipt after the part written before it.
- The built-in projection claims every event type that is not a versioned bundle event (`<owner>.<name>/vN`), plus the qualified alias `runtime.prompt-finished/v1` and `comments.added/v1` and `comments.resolved/v1`; a generic block is drawn only for a versioned type no projector claims.
- `chat.lexical/v1` freezes when the window's first conversation editor is created; a registration after that fails with `frozen`.
- `onBeforeRenderBlockHook` runs for each block after the projection. Until it answers, and when it fails, the block is drawn with `actions: []`; of its answer only `data` and `actions` are taken, and `id`, `type`, `promptId` and `sequence` stay the projector's.
- `onThreadUpdateHook` is called for each live `event` of a held Thread as it arrives, before the 100 ms flush, and for each `updated` record; never for a snapshot or for history. The chat does not await it and swallows its failure.
- [`files`](../../bundles/features/files/README.md) and [`git`](../../bundles/features/git/README.md) observe `chat.hooks/v2.onThreadUpdateHook` and request `files.refresh/v1` after their own tools; `onSandboxActivityHook` does not fire after a finished tool call.
- A tool's calls are drawn through `chat.views/v1.registerToolRenderer`: the element gets one property, `view`, and answers with one DOM event. An input source's prompts are drawn through `chat.views/v1.registerInputRenderer`, keyed by the source's `kind`, or by `bundle:<id>` for a bundle's instructions. The block those elements share is `desktop/ui/collapsible-block`.

## Current behavior and contracts

### The bundles

- The [`chat`](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md) bundle, version 2.0.0, provides `chat.views/v1@1.2.0`, `chat.lexical/v1@1.0.0`, `chat.hooks/v2@2.0.0`, `chat.composer/v1@1.0.0`, `chat.send/v1@1.1.0` and the command `chat.send-prompt/v1@1.0.0` ([manifest.json](../../bundles/desktop/chat/manifest.json)). It has a client part, which draws the conversation and its footer controls, and a desktop part, which holds every Thread's composer draft in the main process ([src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/index.ts), [src/desktop/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/desktop/index.ts)).
- The chat registers the route `/projects/:projectId/threads/:threadId`, a child of `/`, which creates `<chat-view>` with the path's parameters ([src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/index.ts), [src/client/domain/thread-route.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-route.ts)). It defines `<chat-view>`, `<chat-composer>`, `<chat-thread-panel>`, `<chat-block>`, `<chat-readonly-markdown>`, `<chat-thread-tab-icon>` and `<chat-execution-picker>` ([src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/index.ts)). Their properties: `<chat-view projectId threadId>`, `<chat-thread-panel projectId threadId>`, `<chat-thread-tab-icon projectId threadId>`, `<chat-readonly-markdown text>` and `<chat-block block>`; `<chat-composer>` has none ([src/client/elements/chat-elements.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/elements/chat-elements.tsx)).
- Eight contracts are optional ([manifest.json](../../bundles/desktop/chat/manifest.json), [src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/index.ts)). Without `history.snapshots/v1` a Thread opens from the host alone. Without `models/v1` the agent's mark is the bot glyph. Without `settings.models/v1` the footer has no execution picker. Without `workspace.selection/v1` `<chat-composer>` has no Thread and its controls are disabled ([src/client/hooks/use-workspace.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-workspace.ts)). Without `workspace.hooks/v1` no Thread tab opens ([src/client/services/thread-tabs.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/thread-tabs.ts)). `comments.added/v1`, `comments.resolved/v1` and `runtime.prompt-finished/v1` are event types the chat reads when the log holds them.
- The chat holds one `runtime.records/v1` view per Thread on screen: the route's Thread, and the Thread of the selected viewer tab when the chat opened that tab. Without that contract's provider it holds none ([src/client/services/thread-views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/thread-views.ts), [src/client/domain/thread-views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-views.ts)). The records are described in [thread-archive.md](thread-archive.md).
- `<chat-view>` draws nothing until it holds the Thread's record, and draws only the conversation: it has no breadcrumb and no title header. Another Thread is a new editor, keyed by the Thread ([src/client/views/chat-view.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/views/chat-view.tsx)).
- The [`layout`](../../bundles/desktop/layout/README.md) bundle draws the conversation inside its conversation frame, with the conversation footer floating over the frame's bottom; the footer draws the slot `shell.conversation.footer` ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx), [bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/templates/conversation-frame.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/templates/conversation-frame.tsx), [bundles/desktop/layout/src/contracts/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/contracts/index.ts)).

### The Thread's log

- The conversation's history is the Thread's durable event log on the host. The window's Thread chat store is the only reader of that stream: it watches each Thread it holds through `runtime.threads/v1.watchThread` from the last sequence it has, folds the events into that Thread's one ordered log, and projects the log into turns ([src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts), [src/client/api/thread-watch.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/thread-watch.ts), [src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts)).
- The store holds at most 20 Threads, each with its own watch, and drops the least recently viewed first; switching back to a held Thread shows its live projection with nothing replayed. When the [`history`](../../bundles/desktop/history/README.md) bundle is present, a Thread the store opens starts from its local snapshot and subscribes from where that snapshot ends, and the chat hands it every applied batch ([src/client/domain/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-chats.ts), [src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts), [src/client/services/chat-services.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/chat-services.ts)). The snapshots are described in [local-history.md](local-history.md).
- Live events reach the projection in flushes of at most 100 ms per Thread; the first event of a burst lands at once ([src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts)).
- A dropped or unavailable bridge, or a lost host generation, ends a watch silently, and the watch resubscribes from its cursor once the connection reports `connected` again. While the connection reads `connected` but the bridge is still down, the watch resubscribes after 1 s, and again for as long as each try ends the same way. Any other end holds a message as the Thread's error: `The Thread event stream returned an invalid snapshot.`, `The Thread event stream returned an invalid event.`, the host's message when it sent a code and a message, else `The Thread event stream failed.`; the conversation does not draw it ([src/client/api/thread-watch.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/thread-watch.ts)).
- The chat reaches the host through `runtime.threads/v1`: a new prompt goes through `prompt`, a steer through `steer`, a replaced past prompt through `rewind`, a paused prompt is taken up through `resume`, an agent Thread's last prompt is sent again through `retry`, and a waiting queue is resumed through `resumeQueue` ([src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts), [src/client/api/threads-api.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/threads-api.ts)). How the host runs, steers and queues prompts is in [prompt-control.md](prompt-control.md) and [projects-and-threads.md](projects-and-threads.md).

### The transcript

- The transcript is one Lexical editor that holds the Thread's turns and, at its end, the prompt the reader types. Each kind of turn is a node of its own: the reader's turn, another Thread's input, an input of another source, the agent's answer, a reasoning run, a tool call, the author line, an activity summary, a lifecycle marker, a compaction, the queue block, the composer's stack, a queued receipt, a failure, the agent's comment replies and the prompt; the agent's Markdown becomes heading, quote, list, code, link and table nodes; a block another bundle's projector made is a `chat-block-node`, and a fence another bundle draws is a fence node. Nodes and extensions other bundles add through `chat.lexical/v1` come after these ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx)).
- Every block but the prompt is read-only: the caret moves through its text and no editing command changes it. A deletion may empty a block, never remove one ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/read-only-blocks.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/read-only-blocks.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/undeletable-blocks.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/undeletable-blocks.tsx)).
- A reasoning run and a tool call are one caret stop each, an activity summary is a run of stops, and Enter on a focused widget opens or closes it. The author line is furniture the caret skips ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/caret-navigation.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/caret-navigation.tsx)).
- Applying the log to the editor never enters the undo history and never moves the caret or restores the editor's DOM selection, so a streaming update does not take the focus or the selection from a popover or another input. Only the pass that opens the conversation seats the caret in the prompt. The reader's own edits keep at most 50 undo entries per conversation ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/insert-thread-turn-nodes.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/insert-thread-turn-nodes.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx)).
- The reader's turn wears its mark beside its words. The agent wears one mark per stretch of work between two turns the reader or a bundle sent, on that stretch's first answer; tool calls, spawns, inputs relayed from other Threads or background terminals and further answers after it wear none. An author line trails the reader's turn and the last turn of each agent run ([src/client/domain/conversation-sync.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/conversation-sync.ts)).
- The reader's mark is their initials over a tone of their name; the agent's mark is the icon of the model that answered, or a bot glyph when that model has no icon; while the icon is pending, or when its read failed, the mark paints nothing and keeps its box ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/turn-avatar.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/turn-avatar.ts)). A mark is 1.375rem in every pane and stands in the column's left padding, its right edge 0.5rem before the text and its centre on the centre of the block's first line; a block that opens with a heading drops the heading's top margin ([src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)).
- A scroll area narrower than 48rem widens the column's left padding to 2.5rem and its top padding to 1rem, so the mark stands whole inside it ([src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)).
- How tool calls, inputs of other sources and fences are drawn is each owner's: the chat draws an unclaimed tool call as a generic row inside the activity summary ([src/client/services/views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/views.ts)). See [terminal.md](terminal.md), [files.md](files.md), [delegation.md](delegation.md) and [generative-ui.md](generative-ui.md). Comments on the agent's answers, and the agent's replies to them, are described in [comments.md](comments.md); images in a prompt in [attachments.md](attachments.md); compaction rows in [context-compaction.md](context-compaction.md); the text of failure rows in [error-messages.md](error-messages.md).

### Queued prompts and steers

- A prompt the reader sends appears at once as a pending turn, drawn dimmed, until the log accepts it. Acceptance of an input of the reader's own (`source.kind: 'user'`) acknowledges the pending send; a delegated input, a background terminal's result or a bundle's instruction never does ([src/client/domain/projector-input.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector-input.ts), [src/client/domain/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-chats.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/user-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/user-turn-node.tsx)).
- An input the host queues becomes a receipt where the log records it as queued (`prompt.queued`); the full prompt renders separately where its execution starts, once: a resume does not draw it again, and live batches, replay and reload draw the same order. Until it shows, a queued input's text follows each `prompt.edited`. An acceptance that carries no `queued` field renders at acceptance ([src/client/domain/projector-input.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector-input.ts)).
- A receipt reads `Queued <avatar> <author> "<prompt>"` and is a folded collapsible block that opens to the whole input; it keeps the reader's open or closed choice across updates. Consecutive receipts form one `Queued N prompts` block whose list keeps every input's text and author in order; a visible turn between them separates the groups ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/queued-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/queued-turn-node.tsx), [src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts)).
- A steer's words stay drawn as a pending turn until the running prompt takes them in, then render as the reader's turn where `prompt.steered` landed; a steer that goes back to the queue renders as a receipt ([src/client/domain/projector-input.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector-input.ts)).

### The queue block

- The queue block sits immediately above the prompt, as stable non-editable furniture of the editor. Its heading reads `Queue`, and it lists only the enqueued inputs: the running prompt is the transcript's last turn and a paused one is taken up from its marker, so neither is a row ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/queue-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/queue-node.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx), [src/client/domain/queue.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/queue.ts)).
- The block is hidden while the queue has no item and no pause notice. A paused queue shows its notice with Retry; a failed refresh shows a compact notice; a queue that could not be read shows `Queue unavailable` with Retry ([src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx)).
- The rows scroll inside a list of at most `max-h-48`. A row shows the input's author and words; a reader's row shares its avatar and name with the receipts, and an input of another source keeps that source's identity (`Terminal`, `<bundle> · <reason>`, `Parent · <id>`, `Subthread · <id>`, where `<id>` is the first 8 characters of the Thread's id). Clicking a row opens its read-only details in a popover ([src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx), [src/client/domain/queue.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/queue.ts)).
- The arrow keys move through the rows as caret stops between the transcript and the prompt. Enter on a row edits it in the prompt when it is the reader's own and the host marks it editable, and otherwise opens its details. Delete or Backspace removes the selected input ([src/client/hooks/use-queue-caret.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-queue-caret.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/caret-navigation.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/caret-navigation.tsx)).
- While a row is edited, a header above the prompt reads `Editing: <avatar> <author> "<original prompt>"`, and the prompt and the row wear the muted tone at 50 %. The footer's check button and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter save; Escape gives the draft back without touching the run. A save the host refuses keeps the typed edit with its error, and text typed while a save is pending is kept ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/prompt-edit-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/prompt-edit-node.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/edit-queue-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/edit-queue-prompt.tsx), [src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)).

### The composer

- The prompt is the transcript's last block, so the reader writes in the same document they read ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/user-prompt-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/user-prompt-node.tsx)).
- Every send carries the prompt envelope: the comments on the composer's stack (described below) grouped by target in `<comments>`, then the reader's words in `<message>`; either part alone is a whole prompt. A send with an empty prompt still goes out when the stack holds a comment. Where the chat shows a stored prompt, it reads back the words of `<message>` ([src/client/domain/prompt-envelope.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/prompt-envelope.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx)).
- The prompt empties as the send leaves; a send the host refuses puts the reader's words back, and the stack's comments stay on the stack ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx)).
- While a prompt runs, the prompt's keys control it: Cmd/Ctrl+Enter steers it with the words at its next safe point, Tab queues the words for after it, Escape with words aborts its current step and steers it, and Escape without words or Ctrl+C with nothing selected stops it ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx)).
- Cmd/Ctrl+Enter with nothing to send resumes the waiting queue through the same operation as the footer's button. It does nothing while a prompt runs, stops or resumes, when the queue could not be read, when no item waits, or while a queued edit is open, where it saves the edit ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx), [src/client/domain/queue.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/queue.ts)).
- A typed message takes the composer's route: a steer of the running prompt, else a new prompt; a send into a paused, empty queue resumes it first ([src/client/domain/send-route.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/send-route.ts)).
- `Cmd/Ctrl+Enter` has one owner per window, bound through `menus/v1` on the conversation's route to the chat's command `chat.send-prompt/v1`. The command sends for the Thread whose conversation holds the focus, else the route's Thread, wherever the focus is in the window, and is the same send the footer's control makes. Before it reads the stack it dispatches `doric:prompt-send` on the document with `{ threadId, waitUntil }`: a surface holding an unconfirmed note confirms it and hands the promise to `waitUntil`, and the send waits for it ([src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/index.ts), [src/client/services/send-command.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/send-command.ts), [src/contracts/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/contracts/index.ts)).
- Each Thread has its own composer state and its own send signal, so two conversations shown at once never send each other's draft ([src/client/stores/composer.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/composer.ts), [src/client/hooks/use-composer.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-composer.ts)).

### The composer's stack

- A Thread's draft text and its comment stack are one state in the main process, keyed by `(projectId, threadId)` and shared by every window; deactivating the desktop part drops it ([src/desktop/composer-state.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/desktop/composer-state.ts), [src/desktop/composer-service.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/desktop/composer-service.ts), [src/desktop/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/desktop/index.ts)).
- The stack sits inside the prompt, above the reader's words. A window opens one watch per Thread it shows and hands a note's edits to `chat.composer/v1` 150 ms after the last keystroke; an edit still held goes at once when its note loses the focus, on `doric:prompt-send`, before the stack is read back, and on the last release of the Thread's watch. Removing an item drops its held edit, and an echo of an older write never overwrites a note written since ([src/client/stores/composer-stack.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/composer-stack.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/composer-stack.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/composer-stack.tsx), [src/client/services/send-command.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/send-command.ts)).
- Only after the host accepts the prompt does the stack flush: each item's kind runs the flush its owner registered, and the sender writes the durable comments; an item whose write fails goes back on the stack. The chat owns the kind `message`, a comment on a range of an agent's answer ([src/client/services/send.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/send.ts), [src/desktop/composer-state.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/desktop/composer-state.ts), [src/desktop/message-flush.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/desktop/message-flush.ts)). The comments themselves are described in [comments.md](comments.md).

### Lifecycle markers

- A pause (`prompt.paused`) and a resume are markers between the blocks they sit between: quiet separator rows of 12 px text with no icon. A pause reads `Execution paused · the host stopped at HH:MM` (`· the host stopped` with no time), `· the host restarted unexpectedly`, `· you stopped the execution` or `· low storage; free disk space above 10% and resume`; a resume reads `Execution resumed · attempt N`. The caret skips both ([src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts), [src/client/components/molecules/prompt-marker.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/prompt-marker.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/lifecycle-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/lifecycle-turn-node.tsx)).
- A pause that still stands offers a compact icon-only button with the RotateCcw icon and the accessible name `Resume`, which keeps normal click and Tab access ([src/client/components/molecules/prompt-marker.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/prompt-marker.tsx), [src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts)).
- A pause stops standing when a later resume of the same prompt or a terminal status follows it, when the log records a recovery of the prompt after it, or when an input of the reader's own accepted outside the queue follows it. A delegated input and an acceptance marked `queued: true` leave it standing. Live batches, replay and rewind read the same log the same way ([src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts)).
- A recovery (`prompt.resumed`) alone draws no marker: the projection waits for the prompt's next `agent.started` and shows a resume marker only when an earlier execution of that prompt had started. When the transcript's last turn is the pause, that execution turns the pause marker into the resume marker and keeps the pause's line as its tooltip; otherwise the resume marker appears where the execution starts ([src/client/domain/projector-execution.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector-execution.ts), [src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts), [src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts)).
- A prompt closed because its attempts ran out is a failure row in the warning tone, titled `Execution stopped`, that offers Retry ([src/client/domain/error-catalog.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/error-catalog.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/failure-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/failure-turn-node.tsx)).

### The Conversation Trail

- The trail is a `nav` labelled "Conversation trail" in a 26 px column left of the scroll area, centred on the column's height ([src/client/components/molecules/thread-nav-rail.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/thread-nav-rail.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx)).
- It has one handle per accepted prompt the reader wrote, in transcript order, then a last bar labelled "Go to the end". Queued receipts, a parent's instructions, a subthread's results and unaccepted drafts have no handle ([src/client/domain/thread-nav.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-nav.ts), [src/client/hooks/use-thread-nav.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-thread-nav.ts)).
- A handle is 10 px wide. Hovering it widens it to 17 px, and its neighbours step up by distance — 13, 12 and 11 px, brighter the nearer they are; the cascade changes only the handles and ends when the pointer leaves. The active handle is the full primary colour, hover included ([src/client/components/molecules/thread-nav-rail.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/thread-nav-rail.tsx), [src/client/domain/thread-nav.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-nav.ts)).
- Hovering or focusing a handle opens a popover to its right with the prompt's words, or its comments' notes when it has none, over a footer with the reader's avatar and name; the last bar's popover reads "Go to the end" ([src/client/components/molecules/thread-nav-rail.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/thread-nav-rail.tsx), [src/client/domain/thread-nav.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-nav.ts)).
- The active handle is the last prompt whose top is at or above the reading line, 40 % down the viewport, else the first measured one ([src/client/domain/thread-nav.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-nav.ts)).
- Clicking a handle jumps at once to put its prompt's top on the reading line, bounded by the scroll edges, and that handle stays active until the reader scrolls by hand. The destination fills with the muted background, which fades over 500 ms to a 32 % tint; the reader's own scroll, or hovering the block with a mouse or pen, clears it. Only a click starts the effect ([src/client/hooks/use-thread-nav.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-thread-nav.ts), [src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)).
- A wheel or a touch over the trail's column scrolls the conversation ([src/client/hooks/use-thread-nav.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-thread-nav.ts)).

- A projector turns events into blocks, and a block renderer's element, created through `ui/v1`, gets the property `block`. A fence, tool or input element gets the property `view` (`ChatFenceView`, `ChatToolView`, `ChatInputView`), which the chat sets again, as a new object, whenever a field changes; a tool's element is not created again while its call is shown ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/chat-block-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/chat-block-node.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/fence-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/fence-node.tsx), [src/client/components/molecules/view-element.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/view-element.tsx)).
- A fence's key never changes while its turn streams, and its state moves only from `streaming` to `closed` or `unterminated`. `watchFences` gives one Thread's fences of a tag to a surface outside the conversation, and watching never changes the store's Threads or their recency ([src/client/domain/fences.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/fences.ts), [src/client/services/fence-watches.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/fence-watches.ts)).
- A tool's registration alone decides how its calls are drawn: the element, whether a call stands outside the activity summary, and whether its block stays open once settled. Without one, and after its disposal, a call is the chat's generic row inside the activity summary, closed once settled ([src/client/domain/tool-drawing.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/tool-drawing.ts)). An input renderer takes precedence over a block renderer of `chat.delegated-input/v1` for the same prompt ([src/client/domain/drawn-turns.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/drawn-turns.ts)).
- A tool or input element reports the reader's open or close with the event `chat-block-chosen`, which the chat keeps as the block's choice. The conversation's toggle key calls the element's `toggle()` when it defines one; without it, the key sets the opposite of the chat's own answer: for a tool, of a block open while it runs and, once settled, open only with `openByDefault`; for an input, of a block folded once settled ([src/client/components/molecules/view-element.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/view-element.tsx), [src/client/domain/collapsible.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/collapsible.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/input-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/input-turn-node.tsx)).

Blocks of other bundles in the transcript:

- A projector is pure and runs over the whole log on every batch, so replay and rewind run it again; a projector that throws keeps its state and adds nothing for that event. Blocks are ordered by `sequence`, and a versioned event type no projector claims becomes one generic block, drawn by `<chat-block>` as its type, id, time and payload. `<chat-block>` also draws a projector's block whose type has no renderer, as its type, id and data, with no time ([src/client/services/views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/views.ts), [src/client/domain/external-blocks.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/external-blocks.ts), [src/client/components/molecules/generic-block.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/generic-block.tsx)).
- A block of another bundle sits before the first turn opened by a later event, else above the queue and the prompt ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/insert-thread-turn-nodes.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/insert-thread-turn-nodes.tsx)).
- A card inside the conversation can dispatch a `chat:intent` DOM event; the chat runs it as `commands/v1.execute(command, input)` and shows a failure as the command's one error toast, so repeated failures replace each other ([src/client/components/organisms/intent-dispatcher.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/intent-dispatcher.tsx)).
