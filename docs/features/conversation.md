# Feature: Conversation

- Status: `implemented`

## Problem

The reader works with an agent through one Thread at a time. They need to read the agent's answers, reasoning and tool calls as they stream in, write the next prompt in the same place, and steer, queue, pause or stop the work while it runs. A long transcript needs a way to find a past prompt and to come back to the end. The controls that act on the Thread must stay in reach without cutting the transcript short, and other bundles must be able to draw their own events, tools and fences inside the conversation without the conversation knowing them.

## Decisions

- The conversation footer floats over the bottom of its conversation, and the transcript scrolls on behind it. Both footers, the conversation card's and a child Thread's footer in the viewer card, are one band: 44 px high, 8 px over and under its 28 px row, filled with the background at 70 % under a 2 px blur. Each owner's own sheet keys the band on the footer's `data-slot` and sets `--shell-conversation-footer-height` to 2.75rem beside it: the [`layout`](../../bundles/desktop/layout/README.md) bundle for the conversation frame, the chat for `<chat-thread-panel>` ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/templates/conversation-frame.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/templates/conversation-frame.tsx), [bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/styles.css), [src/client/views/chat-view.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/views/chat-view.tsx), [src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)). The class strings are in the chat README's [Styling](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#styling).
- The chat reads `--shell-conversation-footer-height`: the conversation's vertical scrollbar ends that far above the bottom, and the scroll viewport's bottom scroll padding is that height, so a block the browser scrolls into view stops above the band. Unset, the variable counts as 0 and the bar is full height ([src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)).
- The band takes the pointer: no pointer rule is added, so a click, a wheel or a touch on the band goes to the band or its items, and a wheel over the band does not scroll the conversation ([src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)).
- The chat's sheet isolates the scroll area; the band, positioned and later in tree order, paints over it with no `z-index` ([src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)).
- The transcript column keeps a bottom padding of 20rem, so the end of the conversation still scrolls clear of the band ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx)).
- The conversation card draws no bottom padding, so the conversation and its band reach the card's bottom edge ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx)).
- The viewer card draws no bottom padding while its selected tab asks for an open bottom (`openBottom` on a `layout.tabs/v2` tab), and keeps its padding for every other tab ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/templates/workspace-layout.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/templates/workspace-layout.tsx)).
- The caret of a prompt being typed can stop under the floating band; this is accepted as a known limitation.
- The footer bands define no colour: their items draw in the foreground colour and only the separators are muted. The execution picker anchors the left of the band; the other items sit at its right ([src/client/components/organisms/execution-picker.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/execution-picker.tsx)).
- The chat's composer is not part of the transcript: it is the item `composer` of the slot `shell.conversation.footer`, at order 40, which the chat adds through `regions/v1` ([src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/index.ts)).
- The Conversation Trail sits in its own 26 px column left of the conversation's scroll area (8 px padding, a 10 px bar box, 8 px padding), not inside the content's padding, so the scrollbar stays at the pane's right edge. Its bars are left-aligned in their box and grow rightwards on hover, and its popover opens to the right of the handle. When the scroll area is narrower than 48rem, the column's left padding widens to 2.5rem and its top padding to 1rem, so a turn's mark stands whole inside it ([src/client/components/molecules/thread-nav-rail.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/thread-nav-rail.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx), [src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)).
- The chat computes each fence's key `<promptId>/<turnSequence>/<index>` and its state, `streaming`, `closed` or `unterminated` ([src/client/domain/fences.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/fences.ts)). A queue receipt ends no turn: an answer still streaming continues in the same turn, with the receipt after the part written before it ([src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts)).
- The built-in projection claims every event type that is not a versioned bundle event (`<owner>.<name>/vN`), plus `runtime.prompt-finished/v1`, `comments.added/v1` and `comments.resolved/v1`; another bundle's projector cannot claim them, and a generic block is drawn only for a versioned type no projector claims ([src/client/domain/external-blocks.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/external-blocks.ts), [src/client/services/views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/views.ts)).
- `chat.lexical/v1` freezes when the window's first conversation editor is created; a registration after that is refused ([src/client/services/lexical.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/lexical.ts)).
- `onBeforeRenderBlockHook` runs for each block after the projection. Until it answers, and when it fails, the block is drawn with no actions; of its answer only `data` and `actions` are taken, and the block's id, type, prompt and sequence stay the projector's ([src/client/hooks/use-external-blocks.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-external-blocks.ts)).
- `onThreadUpdateHook` is called for each live `event` of a held Thread as it arrives, before the 100 ms flush, and for each `updated` record; never for a snapshot or for history. The chat does not await it and swallows its failure ([src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts), [src/client/services/chat-hooks.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/chat-hooks.ts)).
- `onSandboxActivityHook` does not fire after a finished tool call: a bundle that refreshes after its own tools observes `onThreadUpdateHook` for them ([src/client/services/sandbox-activity.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/sandbox-activity.ts)).
- A tool's calls are drawn by the element its owner registers through `chat.views/v1.registerToolRenderer`, and an input source's prompts by the element registered through `chat.views/v1.registerInputRenderer`, keyed by the source's `kind`, or by `bundle:<id>` for a bundle's instructions. The element gets one property, `view`, and answers with one DOM event. The block those elements share is `CollapsibleBlock` of [`desktop/ui`](../../packages/desktop/README.md#collapsibleblock-blockopen-and-blocktoggled) ([src/client/services/views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/views.ts), [src/client/domain/tool-drawing.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/tool-drawing.ts), [src/client/components/molecules/view-element.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/view-element.tsx)).

## Current behavior and contracts

### The bundles

- The [`chat`](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md) bundle provides the six contracts of [Contracts](#contracts) ([manifest.json](../../bundles/desktop/chat/manifest.json)). It has a client part, which draws the conversation and its footer controls, and a desktop part, which holds every Thread's composer draft in the main process ([src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/index.ts), [src/desktop/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/desktop/index.ts)).
- Through `navigation/v1` the chat registers the route `/projects/:projectId/threads/:threadId`, a child of `/`, which creates `<chat-view>` with the path's parameters ([src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/index.ts), [src/client/domain/thread-route.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-route.ts)). Through `ui/v1` it defines `<chat-view projectId threadId>`, `<chat-composer>`, `<chat-thread-panel projectId threadId>`, `<chat-block>`, `<chat-readonly-markdown>`, `<chat-thread-tab-icon>` and `<chat-execution-picker>`; their properties are in the README's [Elements, route, slot items](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#elements-route-slot-items) ([src/client/elements/chat-elements.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/elements/chat-elements.tsx)).
- `<chat-view>` draws nothing until it holds the Thread's record, and draws only the conversation: it has no breadcrumb and no title header. Another Thread is a new editor, keyed by the Thread ([src/client/views/chat-view.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/views/chat-view.tsx)).
- The [`layout`](../../bundles/desktop/layout/README.md) bundle draws the conversation inside its conversation frame, with the conversation footer floating over the frame's bottom; the footer draws the slot `shell.conversation.footer` ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx), [bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/templates/conversation-frame.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/templates/conversation-frame.tsx)).
- The chat holds one `runtime.records/v1` view per Thread on screen: the route's Thread, and the Thread of the selected viewer tab when the chat opened that tab. Without a provider of that contract it holds none ([src/client/services/thread-views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/thread-views.ts), [src/client/domain/thread-views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-views.ts)). The records are described in [thread-archive.md](thread-archive.md).

### The Thread's log

- The conversation's history is the Thread's durable event log on the host. The window's Thread chat store is the only reader of that stream: it watches each Thread it holds through `runtime.threads/v1.watchThread` from the last sequence it has, folds the events into that Thread's one ordered log, and projects the log into turns ([src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts), [src/client/api/thread-watch.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/thread-watch.ts), [src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts)). The watch's items and the events' bodies are in the [`runtime`](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/README.md) README's [`runtime.threads/v1`](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/README.md#runtimethreadsv1) and [Event streams](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/README.md#event-streams).
- The store holds at most 20 Threads, each with its own watch, and drops the least recently viewed first; switching back to a held Thread shows its live projection with nothing replayed. When the [`history`](../../bundles/desktop/history/README.md) bundle is present (`history.snapshots/v1`), a Thread the store opens starts from its local snapshot and subscribes from where that snapshot ends, and the chat hands it every applied batch ([src/client/domain/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-chats.ts), [src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts), [src/client/services/chat-services.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/chat-services.ts)). The snapshots are described in [local-history.md](local-history.md).
- What each item of the watch does to the held Thread ([src/client/api/thread-watch.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/thread-watch.ts), [src/client/domain/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-chats.ts)):
  - A `snapshot` adds its events of this Thread to the log, and replaces the Thread's record when it carries one.
  - An `event` joins the log. Live events reach the projection in flushes of at most 100 ms per Thread; the first event of a burst lands at once ([src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts)).
  - An `updated` replaces the Thread's record.
  - A `deleted` drops the Thread from the store and releases its watch; the conversation's hook then reads the error `This thread was deleted.` ([src/client/hooks/use-thread-chat.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-thread-chat.ts)).
  - An item naming another Thread is dropped, and a snapshot's events of another Thread are filtered out. The cursor is the highest sequence delivered.
- A snapshot whose events are not a list, or an event with no valid sequence, sets the Thread's error to `The Thread event stream returned an invalid snapshot.` or `The Thread event stream returned an invalid event.` for that one item, and the stream keeps running ([src/client/api/thread-watch.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/thread-watch.ts)).
- How a stream's end is handled ([src/client/api/thread-watch.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/thread-watch.ts)):
  - An end with `disconnected`, `unavailable` or `generation_lost` sets no error. The watch resubscribes from its cursor when [`doric.connection/v1`](../architecture/bundles/contexts.md#shapes-of-the-platform-contracts), the window's connection status, next reports `connected`; when it already reads `connected`, since that status follows a socket of its own and the bridge may still be down, the watch resubscribes after 1 s, and again for as long as each try ends the same way. Without a provider of `doric.connection/v1` the watch does not resubscribe.
  - An end with no error does nothing: no error is set and the watch does not subscribe again.
  - Any other error sets the Thread's error to the host's message when it sent a code and a message, else `The Thread event stream failed.`, and the watch does not subscribe again. A provider without `watchThread` ends the same way.
  - The conversation does not draw the Thread's error.

### From the log to turns

Each event of the log names its prompt, its sequence, its type and its body. The built-in projection turns them into turns in log order ([src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts), [src/client/domain/projector-input.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector-input.ts), [src/client/domain/projector-execution.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector-execution.ts)). The bodies the runtime writes are in the runtime README's [Event streams](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/README.md#event-streams), and those of the agent loop in the [`agent`](../../packages/agent/README.md#agentevent) package's README.

- **Inputs.** `prompt.accepted` records an input's text and source. `prompt.queued` draws the input's queued receipt; `prompt.edited` changes a queued input's text until the input shows. `prompt.started` draws the input as its turn, and `prompt.steered` draws a steer where the running prompt took it in; an acceptance that carries no `queued` field draws the input at acceptance. The turn is the reader's turn for a `user` source, another Thread's input for a parent or a subthread, and an input of another source otherwise ([Queued prompts and steers](#queued-prompts-and-steers)).
- **Answers.** `response.started` names the provider and the model whose icon marks the answer. `text.delta` adds to the prompt's open answer, or opens one; a delta with no text, or whose text is only whitespace, opens none. `reasoning.delta` adds to the open reasoning run, or opens one; a delta with no text opens none. `prompt.finished` sets the prompt's status; when the prompt completed, its `text` replaces the answer's, and when nothing streamed and the host names a text, it becomes the answer.
- **Tools.** `tool.started` opens a running tool call; `tool.finished` and `tool.failed` settle the call with the same call id, with its output or its error.
- **Execution.** `prompt.paused` draws a pause marker when its `reason` is `host_stopped`, `host_restarted`, `storage_low` or `reader_stopped`, and nothing for any other reason ([src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts)); `prompt.resumed` followed by the prompt's next `agent.started` draws a resume marker ([Lifecycle markers](#lifecycle-markers)). `agent.failed` draws a failure row when its error carries a code or a message, and marks the prompt failed; `agent.cancelled` marks it cancelled.
- **Compaction.** `compaction.started` opens a compaction row, and `compaction.finished` or `compaction.failed` closes the open one ([context-compaction.md](context-compaction.md)).
- **Rewind.** `history.truncated` draws nothing; the events between its `afterSequence` and itself are dropped, and the log is projected again from its start.
- **Comments.** `comments.added/v1` joins the reader's turn of its prompt, or the agent's reply block when the agent answers a comment; `comments.resolved/v1` marks a comment resolved ([comments.md](comments.md)). The chat reads both rows in either stored encoding through `commentsAddedOf` and `commentsResolvedOf` of `comments/contracts`, falling back to the row as stored ([src/contracts/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/contracts/index.ts), [src/client/domain/comment-threads.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/comment-threads.ts)).
- Any other versioned type goes to the projectors of other bundles ([Blocks of other bundles](#blocks-of-other-bundles)).

### The host calls

The chat reaches the host through [`runtime`](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/README.md#runtimethreadsv1)'s `runtime.threads/v1` only, through one adapter ([src/client/api/threads-api.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/threads-api.ts), [src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts)). Which control makes which call:

| Control                                                                                                  | Call                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Send or Cmd/Ctrl+Enter with nothing running; Tab while a prompt runs                                     | `prompt`, after `resumeQueue` when the queue is paused and empty; the host queues a prompt sent while another runs |
| `chat.send/v1.send`, in any state                                                                        | `prompt` only: never a steer and never a resume first; the host queues it while a prompt runs                      |
| Cmd/Ctrl+Enter while a prompt runs                                                                       | `steer` of the running prompt                                                                                      |
| Escape with words while a prompt runs                                                                    | `steer` with `interrupt: true`, which also aborts the prompt's current step                                        |
| Stop: the Stop button, Escape without words, Ctrl+C with nothing selected, the agent Thread's pause      | `pause` of the running prompt                                                                                      |
| A pause marker's Resume, the agent Thread's continue on a paused prompt                                  | `resume`                                                                                                           |
| Resume queue, Cmd/Ctrl+Enter with nothing to send, the queue's pause notice, the agent Thread's continue | `resumeQueue`                                                                                                      |
| The failure row's Retry, the agent Thread's retry                                                        | `retry`, which sends the Thread's last prompt again                                                                |
| The queue block, on each change                                                                          | `queue`                                                                                                            |
| Opening a queued input's edit; saving it                                                                 | `queuedPrompt`, then `editQueued` with the `revision` it read                                                      |
| Delete or Backspace on a queue row                                                                       | `removeQueued`                                                                                                     |

- A steer can come back `steered` or `queued`: a queued steer renders as a receipt ([src/client/api/threads-api.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/threads-api.ts)).
- The store and the conversation's hook also offer `rewind`, which replaces a past prompt; no control of the chat calls it ([src/client/hooks/use-thread-chat.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-thread-chat.ts)).
- A failed call reads as one of three fixed messages: `Doric backend is unavailable.` when the bridge or the host generation is lost, `Doric returned an invalid response.` when the answer failed its shape check, and `The request could not be completed.` otherwise. Each call also refuses its own cases before or after the host answers, for example `Enter a prompt before continuing.`, `The thread was not found.`, `The thread cannot perform this operation.`, and a rewind of a busy Thread ([src/client/api/threads-api.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/threads-api.ts)). The codes are in the README's [Errors and failure behavior](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#errors-and-failure-behavior).
- A failed send, steer, retry or resume shows one error toast per Thread, id `thread-send-<threadId>`, worded by the chat's error catalog, with the code `send_failed` ("Prompt not sent") when the error carries none ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx), [src/client/domain/error-catalog.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/error-catalog.ts)). A failed stop shows the toast `thread-stop-<threadId>` ("Could not stop") ([src/client/hooks/use-thread-stop.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-thread-stop.ts)), and a failed queue action the toast `thread-queue-<threadId>` ("Queue not updated") ([src/client/hooks/use-thread-queue.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-thread-queue.ts)).
- How the host runs, steers and queues prompts is in [prompt-control.md](prompt-control.md) and [projects-and-threads.md](projects-and-threads.md).

### The transcript

- The transcript is one Lexical editor that holds the Thread's turns and, at its end, the prompt the reader types. Each kind of turn is a node of its own: the reader's turn, another Thread's input, an input of another source, the agent's answer, a reasoning run, a tool call, the author line, an activity summary, a lifecycle marker, a compaction, the queue block, the composer's stack, a queued receipt, a failure, the agent's comment replies and the prompt; the agent's Markdown becomes heading, quote, list, code, link and table nodes; a block another bundle's projector made is a `chat-block-node`, and a fence another bundle draws is a fence node. Nodes and extensions other bundles add through `chat.lexical/v1` come after these ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx)).
- Every block but the prompt is read-only: the caret moves through its text and no editing command changes it. A deletion may empty a block, never remove one ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/read-only-blocks.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/read-only-blocks.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/undeletable-blocks.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/undeletable-blocks.tsx)).
- A reasoning run and a tool call are one caret stop each, an activity summary is a run of stops, and Enter on a focused widget opens or closes it. The author line is furniture the caret skips ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/caret-navigation.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/caret-navigation.tsx)).
- Applying the log to the editor never enters the undo history and never moves the caret or restores the editor's DOM selection, so a streaming update does not take the focus or the selection from a popover or another input. Only the pass that opens the conversation seats the caret in the prompt. The reader's own edits keep at most 50 undo entries per conversation ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/insert-thread-turn-nodes.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/insert-thread-turn-nodes.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx)).
- The reader's turn wears its mark beside its words. The agent wears one mark per stretch of work between two turns the reader or a bundle sent, on that stretch's first answer; tool calls, spawns, inputs relayed from other Threads or background terminals and further answers after it wear none. An author line trails the reader's turn and the last turn of each agent run ([src/client/domain/conversation-sync.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/conversation-sync.ts)).
- The reader is the fixed name `READER_NAME` of `desktop/ui/identity`, `jao.scheuermann`, on every install: the host does not report who the reader is. The reader's mark is that name's initials over that name's tone, and the reader's turns, receipts, queue rows and stack items carry that name ([packages/desktop/src/identity/identity-mark.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/identity/identity-mark.ts), [`desktop` README](../../packages/desktop/README.md#identity-desktopuiidentity-desktopuireader-avatar)). The agent's mark is the icon of the model that answered, read through `models/v1`, or a bot glyph when that model has no icon or `models/v1` has no provider; while the icon is pending, or when its read failed, the mark paints nothing and keeps its box ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/turn-avatar.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/turn-avatar.ts)). A mark is 1.375rem in every pane and stands in the column's left padding, its right edge 0.5rem before the text and its centre on the centre of the block's first line; a block that opens with a heading drops the heading's top margin ([src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)).
- How tool calls, inputs of other sources and fences are drawn is each owner's: the chat draws an unclaimed tool call as a generic row inside the activity summary ([src/client/services/views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/views.ts)). See [terminal.md](terminal.md), [files.md](files.md), [delegation.md](delegation.md) and [generative-ui.md](generative-ui.md). Comments on the agent's answers, and the agent's replies to them, are described in [comments.md](comments.md); compaction rows in [context-compaction.md](context-compaction.md); the text of failure rows in [error-messages.md](error-messages.md).
- An image pasted or dropped into the prompt is uploaded through [`runtime`](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/README.md#runtimeattachmentsv1)'s `runtime.attachments/v1` and sent with the prompt; a failed upload shows the toast `attach-image-<threadId>-<name>` ("Image not attached"). The flow is in [attachments.md](attachments.md) ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/attach-images.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/attach-images.tsx)).

### Queued prompts and steers

- A prompt the reader sends appears at once as a pending turn, drawn dimmed, until the log accepts it. Acceptance of an input of the reader's own (`source.kind: 'user'`) acknowledges the pending send; a delegated input, a background terminal's result or a bundle's instruction never does ([src/client/domain/projector-input.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector-input.ts), [src/client/domain/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/thread-chats.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/user-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/user-turn-node.tsx)).
- An input the host queues becomes a receipt where the log records it as queued (`prompt.queued`); the full prompt renders separately where its execution starts, once: a resume does not draw it again, and live batches, replay and reload draw the same order. Until it shows, a queued input's text follows each `prompt.edited` ([src/client/domain/projector-input.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector-input.ts)).
- A receipt reads `Queued <avatar> <author> "<prompt>"` and is a folded collapsible block that opens to the whole input; it keeps the reader's open or closed choice across updates. Consecutive receipts form one `Queued N prompts` block whose list keeps every input's text and author in order; a visible turn between them separates the groups ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/queued-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/queued-turn-node.tsx), [src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts)).
- A steer's words stay drawn as a pending turn until the running prompt takes them in, then render as the reader's turn where `prompt.steered` landed; a steer that goes back to the queue renders as a receipt ([src/client/domain/projector-input.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector-input.ts)).

### The queue block

- The queue is read through `runtime.threads/v1.queue`. One read per Thread answers every surface that shows the queue, and it is read again 100 ms after the last of these: a `snapshot`, `updated` or `deleted` update of the Thread; an event of type `prompt.accepted`, `prompt.edited`, `prompt.queued`, `prompt.started`, `prompt.resumed`, `prompt.paused`, `prompt.finished`, `prompt.steered`, `agent.started`, `queue.updated`, `queue.paused`, `queue.resumed` or `history.truncated`; and the connection reporting `connected`. It is read again at once after a successful resume of the queue, removal or stop, and when the window gets the focus. A failed read is tried twice more, and of two answers the one with the higher `revision` stays ([src/client/hooks/use-thread-queue.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-thread-queue.ts), [src/client/domain/queue.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/queue.ts), [src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts)).
- The queue block sits immediately above the prompt, as stable non-editable furniture of the editor. Its heading reads `Queue`, and it lists only the enqueued inputs: the running prompt is the transcript's last turn and a paused one is taken up from its marker, so neither is a row ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/queue-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/queue-node.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx), [src/client/domain/queue.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/queue.ts)).
- What the block shows ([src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx)):
  - Nothing while the queue has no item and no `error`.
  - A paused queue's notice, which shows the queue's `error`, code `queue_paused` ("Execution paused") when it carries none, with a Retry that resumes the queue through `resumeQueue`.
  - After a failed read of a queue already read, a compact notice, code `queue_refresh_failed` ("Queue not refreshed"), with a Retry that reads the queue again.
  - When the queue has never been read and the read failed, `Queue unavailable` with a Retry that reads it again.
- The rows scroll inside a list of at most 12rem. A row shows the input's author and words. A reader's row shares the reader's name and avatar with the receipts. Another source's row shows the queue item's `label` when it has one, else `Terminal`, `<bundle> · <reason>` (`bundle` and `instruction` when either is missing), or `Parent · <id>` or `Subthread · <id>`, where `<id>` is the first 8 characters of the Thread's id, or `unknown`. Clicking a row opens its read-only details, read from the log, in a popover ([src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx), [src/client/domain/queue.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/queue.ts)).
- The arrow keys move through the rows as caret stops between the transcript and the prompt. Enter on a row edits it in the prompt when it is the reader's own and the host marks it editable, and otherwise opens its details. Delete or Backspace removes the selected input ([src/client/hooks/use-queue-caret.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-queue-caret.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/caret-navigation.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/caret-navigation.tsx)).
- Opening an edit reads the input through `queuedPrompt`. While another input's edit is open, the toast `Save the current edit or press Escape before editing another prompt.` (id `queue-edit-hint-<threadId>`) shows and nothing opens; Enter on the row already edited puts the caret at the prompt's end. Both of the following show the toast `queue-edit-<threadId>`. An answer the host no longer marks editable shows `This prompt can no longer be edited.` with the code `conflict`, so the toast is titled "Not possible right now". A failed read shows its own error, titled by its code, or `queue_edit_failed` ("Prompt not edited") when the error carries no code ([packages/desktop/src/error-copy.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/error-copy.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/edit-queue-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/edit-queue-prompt.tsx), [src/client/domain/error-catalog.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/error-catalog.ts)).
- While a row is edited, a header above the prompt reads `Editing: <avatar> <author> "<original prompt>"`, and the prompt and the row wear the muted tone at 50 %. The footer's check button and Cmd/Ctrl+Enter save; Escape gives the draft back without touching the run. A save the host refuses keeps the typed edit with its error, and text typed while a save is pending is kept ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/prompt-edit-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/prompt-edit-node.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/edit-queue-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/edit-queue-prompt.tsx), [src/client/styles.css](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/styles.css)).

### The composer

- The prompt is the transcript's last block, so the reader writes in the same document they read ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/user-prompt-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/user-prompt-node.tsx)).
- Every send carries the prompt envelope: the comments on the composer's stack grouped by target in `<comments>`, then the reader's words in `<message>`; either part alone is a whole prompt. A send with an empty prompt still goes out when the stack holds a comment. Where the chat shows a stored prompt, it reads back the words of `<message>` ([src/client/domain/prompt-envelope.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/prompt-envelope.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx)). The envelope's grammar is in the README's [The prompt envelope](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#the-prompt-envelope).
- The prompt empties as the send leaves; a send the host refuses puts the reader's words back and shows the send toast ([The host calls](#the-host-calls)), and the stack's comments stay on the stack ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx)).
- While a prompt runs, the prompt's keys control it: Cmd/Ctrl+Enter steers it with the words at its next safe point, Tab queues the words for after it, Escape with words aborts its current step and steers it, and Escape without words or Ctrl+C with nothing selected stops it ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx)).
- Cmd/Ctrl+Enter with nothing to send resumes the waiting queue through the same operation as the footer's button. It does nothing while a prompt runs, stops or resumes, when the queue could not be read, when no item waits, or while a queued edit is open, where it saves the edit ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/send-prompt.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx), [src/client/domain/queue.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/queue.ts)).
- A typed message takes the composer's route: a steer of the running prompt, else a new prompt; a send into a paused, empty queue resumes it first ([src/client/domain/send-route.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/send-route.ts)).
- `Cmd/Ctrl+Enter` has one owner per window: the chat binds it through `menus/v1` on the conversation's route to its command `chat.send-prompt/v1`, registered through `commands/v1`; the binding stays after the chat's deactivation. The command sends for the Thread whose conversation holds the focus, else the route's Thread, wherever the focus is in the window, and is the same send the footer's control makes. Before it reads the stack it dispatches `doric:prompt-send` on the document with `{ threadId, waitUntil }`: a surface holding an unconfirmed note confirms it and hands the promise to `waitUntil`, and the send waits for it ([src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/index.ts), [src/client/services/send-command.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/send-command.ts), [src/contracts/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/contracts/index.ts)).
- Each Thread has its own composer state and its own send signal, so two conversations shown at once never send each other's draft ([src/client/stores/composer.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/composer.ts), [src/client/hooks/use-composer.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-composer.ts)).

### The composer's draft and stack

- A Thread's draft text and its comment stack are one state in the main process, `chat.composer/v1`, keyed by `(projectId, threadId)` and shared by every window; deactivating the desktop part drops it ([src/shared/composer-state.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/shared/composer-state.ts), [src/desktop/composer-service.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/desktop/composer-service.ts), [src/desktop/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/desktop/index.ts)).
- A window writes the prompt's text through `chat.composer/v1.setText` 300 ms after the last keystroke. A prompt that opens empty reads the draft back and shows its words, so a Thread opened again, in this window or another, opens with the words it was left with; a prompt that already holds words keeps them ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/composer-draft.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/composer-draft.tsx)).
- The stack sits inside the prompt, above the reader's words. A window opens one watch per Thread it shows and hands a note's edits to `chat.composer/v1` 150 ms after the last keystroke; an edit still held goes at once when its note loses the focus, on `doric:prompt-send`, before the stack is read back, and on the last release of the Thread's watch. Removing an item drops its held edit, and an echo of an older write never overwrites a note written since ([src/client/stores/composer-stack.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/composer-stack.ts), [src/client/components/organisms/composer-stack.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/composer-stack.tsx), [src/client/services/send-command.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/send-command.ts)).
- Only after the host accepts the prompt, the sending window calls `chat.composer/v1.flush` with the accepted prompt's id: each item's kind runs the flush its owner registered, and the window writes each answered comment through `comments/v1.add` of the [`comments`](../../bundles/features/comments/README.md) bundle, in the accepted prompt's scope, so the comment events land in that prompt's log. The write is idempotent on the item's id; an item whose write fails is logged and goes back on the stack under the same id. The draft text then clears in every window ([src/client/api/composer.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/api/composer.ts), [src/client/services/send.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/send.ts), [src/shared/composer-state.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/shared/composer-state.ts)).
- The chat owns the flush of the kind `message`, a comment on a range of an agent's answer ([src/shared/message-flush.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/shared/message-flush.ts)); the [`papercraft`](../../bundles/features/papercraft/README.md) bundle owns the kind `papercraft-program` ([bundles/features/papercraft/src/desktop/index.ts](../../bundles/features/papercraft/src/desktop/index.ts)). The comments themselves are described in [comments.md](comments.md).

### Lifecycle markers

- A pause (`prompt.paused`) and a resume are markers between the blocks they sit between: quiet separator rows of 12 px text with no icon. A pause reads `Execution paused · the host stopped at HH:MM` (`· the host stopped` with no time), `· the host restarted unexpectedly`, `· you stopped the execution` or `· low storage; free disk space above 10% and resume`; a resume reads `Execution resumed · attempt N`. The caret skips both ([src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts), [src/client/components/molecules/prompt-marker.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/prompt-marker.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/lifecycle-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/lifecycle-turn-node.tsx)).
- A pause that still stands offers a compact icon-only button with the RotateCcw icon and the accessible name `Resume`, which keeps normal click and Tab access and takes the prompt up through `resume` ([src/client/components/molecules/prompt-marker.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/prompt-marker.tsx), [src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts)).
- A pause stops standing when a later resume of the same prompt or a terminal status follows it, when the log records a recovery of the prompt after it, or when an input of the reader's own accepted outside the queue follows it. A delegated input and an acceptance marked `queued: true` leave it standing. Live batches, replay and rewind read the same log the same way ([src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts)).
- A recovery (`prompt.resumed`) alone draws no marker: the projection waits for the prompt's next `agent.started` and shows a resume marker only when an earlier execution of that prompt had started. When the transcript's last turn is the pause, that execution turns the pause marker into the resume marker and keeps the pause's line as its tooltip; otherwise the resume marker appears where the execution starts ([src/client/domain/projector-execution.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector-execution.ts), [src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts), [src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/prompt-lifecycle.ts)).

### Failure rows and Retry

- A failure row is the notice of an `agent.failed` error, worded by the chat's error catalog, with the code `prompt_failed` ("Prompt failed") when the error carries none ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/failure-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/failure-turn-node.tsx), [src/client/domain/error-catalog.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/error-catalog.ts)).
- A prompt the host closed after restarting three times during it carries the code `resume_exhausted`: its row, in the warning tone, is titled `Execution stopped` and reads `The host restarted three times during this prompt, so the work may be incomplete; retry to send the prompt again.` ([src/client/domain/error-catalog.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/error-catalog.ts)).
- Retry is offered on any Thread, the reader's or an agent's, on the failure rows of one prompt only, the prompt of the last failure row, while the Thread is not running, no pause stands, the queue is not paused, the last prompt ended in a failure, and the reader has written words in the conversation. The row shows Retry only when the catalog marks its code retryable, as `prompt_failed` and `resume_exhausted` are. Retry calls `runtime.threads/v1.retry`, and its failure shows the send toast ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx), [src/client/domain/agent-control.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/agent-control.ts), [src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/stores/thread-chats.ts), [packages/desktop/src/error-notice.tsx](../../packages/desktop/src/error-notice.tsx)).

### Contracts

The contracts module is [src/contracts/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/contracts/index.ts); the full shape of each contract the chat provides, with its members, schemas and errors, is in the README's [Contracts provided](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#contracts-provided).

What the chat provides ([manifest.json](../../bundles/desktop/chat/manifest.json)):

- [`chat.views/v1`](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#chatviewsv1-client-120) (client): lets other bundles draw inside the conversation: projectors for their event types, renderers for block types, fences, tools and inputs, and `watchFences` for a surface outside the conversation ([Blocks of other bundles](#blocks-of-other-bundles)).
- [`chat.lexical/v1`](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#chatlexicalv1-client-100) (client): lets other bundles add Lexical nodes and extensions to the conversation editor, until it freezes ([Decisions](#decisions)).

- [`chat.composer/v1`](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#chatcomposerv1-desktopmain-100) (desktopMain): one Thread's draft and stack, and the flushes of stack kinds ([The composer's draft and stack](#the-composers-draft-and-stack)).
- [`chat.send/v1`](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#chatsendv1-client-110) (client): `send` reads a Thread's held draft and stack from `chat.composer/v1`, builds the same envelope as the composer, and sends it through `runtime.threads/v1.prompt`, whatever the Thread's state: unlike the composer's route, it never steers a running prompt, which the host then queues it behind, and never resumes a paused, empty queue first. It also dispatches no `doric:prompt-send`, so a surface holding an unconfirmed note is not asked to confirm it before the send; only the note edits this window's stack still holds are written before the draft is read ([src/client/services/send.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/send.ts), [src/client/services/send-command.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/send-command.ts)). It answers `accepted`, `empty` or `refused`; a refused send leaves the draft as it is, and an accepted one flushes the stack as the composer's send does. The [`papercraft`](../../bundles/features/papercraft/README.md) canvas sends with it. `sendAction` sends the text of a control the agent drew, in a `<ui-action program="…">` envelope, by the composer's route, and never touches the draft or the stack; it answers `refused` for an unknown Thread, one of another Project or an agent's, or when the route is not taken, and rejects an input that fails its schema. The [`openui`](../../bundles/features/openui/README.md) bundle calls it ([generative-ui.md](generative-ui.md)).
- [`chat.send-prompt/v1`](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#chatsend-promptv1-command-100) (command): the command the window's `CmdOrCtrl+Enter` dispatches on the conversation's route ([The composer](#the-composer)).

### Hooks

`chat.hooks/v2` ([src/client/services/chat-hooks.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/chat-hooks.ts)); the payloads are in the README's [`chat.hooks/v2`](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#chathooksv2-client-200), and how hooks fire in [hooks.md](../architecture/bundles/hooks.md).

- `onMountHook` fires when a conversation opens on a Thread, and `onUnmountHook` when it closes or moves to another Thread ([src/client/hooks/use-mount-hooks.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-mount-hooks.ts)).
- `onSandboxActivityHook` fires with a Project when the chat starts to hold one of its Threads, or a held Thread's working directory changes, or its state changes to anything but `running`; once per Project per store change ([src/client/services/sandbox-activity.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/sandbox-activity.ts)).
- `onThreadUpdateHook` fires for each live event and each `updated` record of a held Thread ([Decisions](#decisions)).
- `onBeforeRenderBlockHook` transforms each block of another bundle before it is drawn ([Decisions](#decisions)).
- Who observes them: [`files`](../../bundles/features/files/README.md) refreshes a Project on `onSandboxActivityHook`, and [`files`](../../bundles/features/files/README.md) and [`git`](../../bundles/features/git/README.md) request `files.refresh/v1` after their own tools on `onThreadUpdateHook` ([bundles/features/files/src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/features/files/src/client/index.ts), [bundles/features/git/src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/features/git/src/client/index.ts)). [`notifications`](../../bundles/desktop/notifications/README.md) reports live events from `onThreadUpdateHook`, and [`openui`](../../bundles/features/openui/README.md) follows the changes of the agent's answers through it ([bundles/desktop/notifications/src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/desktop/notifications/src/client/index.ts), [bundles/features/openui/src/client/answer-changes.ts](../../bundles/features/openui/src/client/answer-changes.ts)).

### Blocks of other bundles

How a bundle draws inside the conversation through `chat.views/v1`; each spec and view is in the README's [`chat.views/v1`](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#chatviewsv1-client-120) ([src/client/services/views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/views.ts)).

- A projector turns the events of the types it claims into blocks. It is pure and runs over the whole log on every batch, so replay and rewind run it again; a projector that throws keeps its state and adds nothing for that event. Blocks are ordered by `sequence`, and a versioned event type no projector claims becomes one generic block, drawn by `<chat-block>` as its type, id, time and payload. `<chat-block>` also draws a projector's block whose type has no renderer, as its type, id and data, with no time ([src/client/services/views.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/views.ts), [src/client/domain/external-blocks.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/external-blocks.ts), [src/client/components/molecules/generic-block.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/generic-block.tsx)).
- A block renderer's element, created through `ui/v1`, gets the property `block`. A fence, tool or input element gets the property `view`, which the chat sets again, as a new object, whenever a field changes; a tool's element is not created again while its call is shown ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/chat-block-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/chat-block-node.tsx), [src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/fence-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/fence-node.tsx), [src/client/components/molecules/view-element.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/view-element.tsx)).
- Every fence of an agent turn whose info string's first word is a registered tag becomes that tag's element instead of a code block, one per fence key. A fence's key never changes while its turn streams, and its state moves only from `streaming` to `closed` or `unterminated`. `watchFences` gives one Thread's fences of a tag to a surface outside the conversation, and watching never changes the store's Threads or their recency ([src/client/domain/fences.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/fences.ts), [src/client/services/fence-watches.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/services/fence-watches.ts)).
- A tool's registration alone decides how its calls are drawn: the element, whether a call stands outside the activity summary, and whether its block stays open once settled. Without one, and after its disposal, a call is the chat's generic row inside the activity summary, closed once settled ([src/client/domain/tool-drawing.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/tool-drawing.ts)). An input renderer takes precedence over a block renderer of `chat.delegated-input/v1` for the same prompt ([src/client/domain/drawn-turns.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/drawn-turns.ts)).
- A tool or input element reports the reader's open or close with a DOM event, which the chat keeps as the block's choice, and the conversation's toggle key calls the element's `toggle()` when it defines one ([src/client/components/molecules/view-element.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/view-element.tsx), [src/client/domain/collapsible.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/collapsible.ts)).
- Another Thread's input in this one, a parent's instruction or a subthread's result, is the block type `chat.delegated-input/v1`; without a renderer the chat draws the block itself ([src/client/domain/delegated.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/delegated.ts)). Its shape is in the README's [Block types, DOM events and constants](../../bundles/desktop/chat/README.md#block-types-dom-events-and-constants).
- A block of another bundle sits before the first turn opened by a later event, else above the queue and the prompt ([src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/insert-thread-turn-nodes.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/insert-thread-turn-nodes.tsx)).
- A card inside the conversation can dispatch a `chat:intent` DOM event; the chat runs it as `commands/v1.execute(command, input)` and shows a failure as the command's one error toast, so repeated failures replace each other ([src/client/components/organisms/intent-dispatcher.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/intent-dispatcher.tsx)).
