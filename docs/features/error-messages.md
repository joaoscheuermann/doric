# Feature: error messages

- Status: `implemented`

## Problem

A failure in the desktop window must tell the reader what failed, why, and what they can do next, in the same words and the same shape wherever it happens: in the conversation, in Settings, in the workspace sidebar and dashboard, and in a toast after a background action. The reader also needs the technical detail the model or the host saw (the error code and its chain of causes) without having it in their face, a way to copy it, and a Retry only where retrying can help. A bundle that could not start in the window must be reported rather than leave an empty region with no explanation.

## Decisions

- The rules of this feature cover the desktop UI bundles `chat`, `settings`, `layout`, `workspace` and `claude-login`, the platform's `ElementHost`, and the tool-call drawings of `files`, `default`, `threads` and `terminal` in the conversation.
- In those, one contextual notice, `ErrorNotice`, draws every failure where it happens: an icon, a title from the error's code, a one-line explanation, its actions and collapsed technical details. A toast is used only for a background action, and every failure toast has an id. Form validation stays inline under its field.
- A notice offers two actions only: Retry and Copy details.
- Every text a notice or toast shows is in English, including the compaction row and the host's message for a prompt it stopped resuming.
- `ErrorNotice` and `toastError` are the platform module `desktop/ui/error-notice`. The error value (`ErrorInfo`, `ErrorCause`), its builders (`errorInfo`, `errorFrom`, `sameErrorInfo`), `ClientError` and the catalog types (`ErrorCatalog`, `ErrorCatalogEntry`) are in `desktop/reads`, which each client part embeds; they are not platform API, and copies exchange only plain data, never checked with `instanceof`.
- The error catalog is split. The platform holds the entries of the codes the platform and the shared packages define, worded without naming a bundle. Each bundle holds the entries of its own codes and its own wording for shared codes, and passes its catalog to the notice, which merges it over the shared entries.
- The title comes from the first level of the cause chain whose code has an entry, an `internal` level skipped; the explanation comes from that entry, else from that level's own message, else the outer message.
- Retry shows only when the surface passes `onRetry`, the entry is retryable and the error does not say `retryable: false`. Surfaces pass it only for reads, for idempotent writes (the execution picker's write, the queue's resume) and for the failed-prompt block. Copy details shows on every notice.
- Stacks, `ContractError.details`, `diagnostic` and request payloads never enter `ErrorInfo`.
- Tool failures and compaction failures carry their code and structured cause chain to the window; the settings adapters keep the contract error's code and cause.
- A workspace request failure is shown once, and the inline name editor shows no toast.
- The window has one notice for all of its quarantined bundles. When the `layout` itself is quarantined nothing draws it, and the settings window has none.
- A bundle that owns a tool draws the failure of that tool's calls in the conversation, with the shared notice and its own catalog.
- Outside those rules, with their own presentation: the OpenUI errors card, the splash failure screens, the platform's read-failure toast (`useReadError`, used by `files` and `git`), and every failure surface of the feature bundles [`git`](../../bundles/features/git/README.md), [`files`](../../bundles/features/files/README.md), [`terminal`](../../bundles/features/terminal/README.md), [`mcp`](../../bundles/features/mcp/README.md), [`papercraft`](../../bundles/features/papercraft/README.md) and [`usage`](../../bundles/features/usage/README.md) other than the tool-call drawings named above.

## Current behavior and contracts

### The notice

- `ErrorNotice` ([packages/desktop/src/error-notice.tsx](../../packages/desktop/src/error-notice.tsx), the platform module `desktop/ui/error-notice`; its props and signature are in the [`desktop` README](../../packages/desktop/README.md#the-error-notice-desktopuierror-notice); see [platform packages](../architecture/bundles/platform-packages.md)) takes an `ErrorInfo` and, optionally, `variant`, `icon`, `catalog`, `fallbackCode`, `context`, `onRetry`, `retrying` and `announce`. Variants: `block` (the default, a thin card), `compact` (no card, for rows and toasts) and `inline` (row 1 only, no explanation and no details).
- Row 1 holds the tone icon (or the given `icon`), the title on one line truncated with an ellipsis, an icon-only Retry (disabled and spinning while `retrying`) and Copy details. Row 2 holds the muted explanation and an underlined `Details` link (`Hide details` when open) that reveals the details below a hairline, closed on first render.
- The details list `Code`, `Provider`, `Status`, `Message`, one `Caused by:` line per cause (with its code, message, provider and status), one line per `context` entry and `Time` when known. Copy details writes the title, the explanation and the details to the clipboard and reports `Copied` or `Copy failed` for 1 500 ms.
- With `announce`, a notice is `role="alert"` the first time the window shows its key (code, message, time and context), and `role="group"` after; the window remembers the last 200 keys. A notice never moves focus and never throws while rendering.
- The words come from `describeError` ([packages/desktop/src/error-copy.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/error-copy.ts)). It merges the surface's catalog over `sharedErrorCatalog`, the bundle's entry winning. The headline is the first level of the error and its causes whose code has an entry and is not `internal`; else `fallbackCode`'s entry titles the outermost level; else the first `internal` level; else the `unknown` entry (`Something went wrong`). The explanation is the entry's, with the headline level's provider (else the outer provider, else `the provider`) in place of `{provider}`, else the first line of the headline level's message, else of the outer message, at most 200 characters (a longer line is cut and ends with `…`). Retry is allowed when the entry is retryable and the headline level does not say `retryable: false`; with the `unknown` fallback it follows the level's own `retryable`, `true` by default. The tone is the entry's, `error` by default.
- `sharedErrorCatalog` ([packages/desktop/src/error-copy.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/error-copy.ts)) holds the entries of the codes of the shared packages: transport (`unavailable`, `disconnected`, … as `Doric host unavailable`), contract errors (`invalid_input`, `not_found`, `conflict`, `revision_conflict`, `limit_exceeded`), credentials, provider errors (`rate_limit_error`, `overloaded_error`, `auth_failed`, …), model and agent errors, and tool registry errors (`handler_failed` as `Tool failed`, `invalid_payload`, `unknown_tool`, `invalid_schema`).

### The error value and the catalogs

- `ErrorInfo` ([packages/desktop/src/reads/errors.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/reads/errors.ts), exported by `desktop/reads`; its shape, its builders and the catalog types are in the [`desktop` README](../../packages/desktop/README.md#the-error-value)) is plain JSON: the outermost `code` (`''` when none), `provider`, `status`, `message`, `retryable`, at most 7 `causes` (each with optional `code`, `provider`, `status`, `retryable` and its `message`) and the time `at`. A cause whose message is empty or contained in a message above it, and whose code is absent or repeats a code above it, is left out.
- `errorInfo` reads any thrown or stored value into an `ErrorInfo` and never throws. Each level reads `code`, `provider`, `status`, `retryable` and `message`, at the top or under `data`, and the next level is `cause`; at most 8 levels are read. An outermost `handler_failed` level with a cause is skipped, so the tool's own error is the outermost level and the reader sees the reason the model read. `errorFrom(code, message, cause)` builds an `ErrorInfo` whose causes are `cause`'s chain. `sameErrorInfo(a, b)` tells whether two values carry the same code, provider, status, message, `retryable` and causes, ignoring `at`. `ClientError` is the rejection the desktop adapters throw: a code, a message and the original error as `cause`.
- A catalog entry (`ErrorCatalogEntry`) has a one-line English `title`, an optional one-sentence `explanation`, `retryable` and an optional `tone` (`error` or `warning`). Each bundle's own codes live in its own catalog, which it passes to every notice and toast it draws:

### Toasts

- `toastError({ id, error, catalog?, fallbackCode?, context? })` ([packages/desktop/src/error-notice.tsx](../../packages/desktop/src/error-notice.tsx); see the [`desktop` README](../../packages/desktop/README.md#the-error-notice-desktopuierror-notice)) draws a `compact` notice in a Sonner custom toast for 4 000 ms, kept open while its details are open. A later call with the same id replaces it, so toasts never stack per id. It needs the window's `Toaster`, which the `layout` frame and the settings page mount.
- Each success toast has an id of its own and first dismisses its failure toast:
  - `settings`: `config-save-failed` / `config-save` ([bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/hooks/use-config-autosave.ts](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/hooks/use-config-autosave.ts)) and `credentials-write-failed` / `credentials-write` ([bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/settings-credentials.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/settings-credentials.tsx)); the Dashboard's provider page uses `provider-open-site` when the provider's site does not open ([bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard-provider-page.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard-provider-page.tsx)).
  - `claude-login`: the Claude sign-in drawn in the provider page uses `claude-login-failed` (fallback `sign_in_failed`) / `claude-login`. The host stores the login when the sign-in finishes ([bundles/features/claude-login/src/host/login.ts](../../bundles/features/claude-login/src/host/login.ts)). A sign-in that finishes after its element is gone does not hand the credential to the provider page and shows, in place of the success toast, the info toast `claude-login-finished`, `Claude sign-in finished after its page closed; sign in again from the provider page to use it.` ([bundles/features/claude-login/src/client/use-claude-login.ts](../../bundles/features/claude-login/src/client/use-claude-login.ts)).
  - `chat`: `thread-send-<threadId>` ([conversation/index.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/index.tsx)), `thread-stop-<threadId>` ([use-thread-stop.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-thread-stop.ts)), `thread-queue-<threadId>` ([use-thread-queue.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/hooks/use-thread-queue.ts)), `chat-intent-<command>` ([intent-dispatcher.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/intent-dispatcher.tsx)), `queue-edit-<threadId>` ([edit-queue-prompt.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/edit-queue-prompt.tsx)) and `attach-image-<threadId>-<name>` ([attach-images.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/plugins/attach-images.tsx)). A hint that is not a failure keeps its own id, `queue-edit-hint-<threadId>`. The compaction row's Copy shows a plain success or failure toast (`Could not copy the summary.`), not a notice, under the id `compaction-summary-copy` ([compaction-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/compaction-turn-node.tsx)).
- The conversation's `terminal` row shows two failures as a plain `toast.error(message)` with no id and no `ErrorNotice`: a failed Stop, and an error the terminal stream reports while the row follows the Project. Both come from the `terminal` store ([bundles/features/terminal/src/client/store.ts](../../bundles/features/terminal/src/client/store.ts)), whose `toastError` option the client wires to `toast.error` ([bundles/features/terminal/src/client/index.ts](../../bundles/features/terminal/src/client/index.ts)).
- Outside this feature's rules, failures show as plain Sonner toasts: the `terminal` store's failed create and rename, the client's failed open (`Could not open terminal.` when the error has no message, [index.ts](../../bundles/features/terminal/src/client/index.ts)) and the terminal rows' failed command copy ([elements.tsx](../../bundles/features/terminal/src/client/elements.tsx)), all with no id; `git`'s failed copy of a working-directory value and failed branch switch, with no id ([workspace-cwd.tsx](../../bundles/features/git/src/client/cwd/workspace-cwd.tsx), [use-branches.ts](../../bundles/features/git/src/client/hooks/use-branches.ts)); `mcp`'s failures, each with an id; and the read failures of `files` and `git` panels, which `ReadFeedback` shows through `useReadError` ([packages/desktop/src/panels/use-read-error.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/panels/use-read-error.ts), `desktop/ui/panels`) as one `Unable to update` toast per surface, with the error as its description and a `Try again` action, for 8 000 ms, dismissed by the next successful read.

### Conversation

- The chat builds each failure from the stored events: `tool.failed` sets the call's error, `agent.failed` adds a failure block when its error names a code or a message, and `compaction.failed` sets the compaction row's error with code `summarizer_failed` or `unsafe_boundary`, keeping the carried error when its code is that one and otherwise wrapping it as the cause. A `compaction.failed` without `error` shows its code and no causes ([bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/domain/projector.ts)).
- A run the `default` executor refuses because the Thread's stored provider or model is unavailable fails with `thread_execution_unavailable` ([bundles/runtime/default/src/host/executor.ts](../../bundles/runtime/default/src/host/executor.ts)), shown as the failed-prompt block `Model unavailable` with the executor's message.
- A failed prompt is a `block` notice with fallback `prompt_failed` and the context `Thread` and `Prompt`. Its Retry, which calls the Thread's retry, shows only on the last prompt's failure while the agent control's status is `failed` and it can retry ([bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/failure-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/failure-turn-node.tsx)).
- A failed compaction is a `compact` notice inside the compaction separator ([bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/compaction-turn-node.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/conversation/nodes/compaction-turn-node.tsx)). The compaction row's text and its numbers (`en-US`) are English. A run that the [`runtime`](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/README.md) stops after three host restarts fails with `resume_exhausted` and an English message saying the work may be incomplete ([bundles/runtime/runtime/src/host/domain/prompts.ts](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/src/host/domain/prompts.ts)), which the chat titles `Execution stopped`.
- `compaction.failed` carries `{ code, error? }` (`executor/v1` 1.5.0, [bundles/runtime/runtime/src/contracts/executor.ts](../../bundles/runtime/runtime/src/contracts/executor.ts)); the `default` executor records the summarizer's error in it ([bundles/runtime/default/src/host/compact.ts](../../bundles/runtime/default/src/host/compact.ts)).
- A failed tool call shows a `compact` notice with fallback `tool_failed` and the context `Tool`. The chat draws it in its generic tool row ([bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/tool-item.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/molecules/tool-item.tsx)). A bundle that registers a tool's drawing through `chat.views/v1` receives the call's whole `ErrorInfo` in its `view` and draws the notice itself, with its own catalog: `files` for `edit` and `write` (`EditToolItem`, [bundles/features/files/src/client/chat/edit-call.tsx](../../bundles/features/files/src/client/chat/edit-call.tsx)), `default` for `load_skill` ([bundles/runtime/default/src/client/skill-call.tsx](../../bundles/runtime/default/src/client/skill-call.tsx)), `threads` for `thread-spawn` ([bundles/features/threads/src/client/spawn-call.tsx](../../bundles/features/threads/src/client/spawn-call.tsx)) and `terminal` for `terminal` ([bundles/features/terminal/src/client/chat/terminal-call.tsx](../../bundles/features/terminal/src/client/chat/terminal-call.tsx)).
- The queue shows a paused queue as an announced notice whose Retry resumes it, and a failed refresh as a `compact` notice whose Retry reads it again ([bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/thread-queue.tsx)).
- The footer execution picker shows its failures as announced `inline` notices ([bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/execution-picker.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/chat/src/client/components/organisms/execution-picker.tsx); see [per-thread model](per-thread-model.md)). A write the runtime refuses shows on its own Thread as `Model not accepted` or `Thread not found` with the runtime's message and no Retry. A write whose call rejects is titled from the rejection's code by the catalogs, else `Model not accepted`; its Retry, which writes the same execution again, shows only when that code's entry is retryable (a lost host connection shows `Doric host unavailable` with Retry). A failed read of `settings.models/v1` is titled from its code by the catalogs, else `Settings not loaded`; its Retry, which reads it again, shows only when the entry that titles it is retryable.

### Settings

- The settings adapters reject with `SettingsApiError`, a `ClientError` that keeps the host's contract error code and, except for `internal`, the host's error as its cause ([bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/api/settings-api.ts](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/api/settings-api.ts)).
- Each read failure is an announced notice with Retry: the provider kinds in the settings window (`block`, [settings-providers.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/settings-providers.tsx)), the credentials list ([settings-credentials.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/settings-credentials.tsx)), the tools ([settings-tools.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/settings-tools.tsx)), the model catalog (`compact`, [provider-catalog-models.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/provider-catalog-models.tsx), [provider-model-picker.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/provider-model-picker.tsx)), and the provider kinds or credentials on the Dashboard's providers group (`compact`, [dashboard-providers.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard-providers.tsx)). While a Retry reads again, the last failure stays on screen with its Retry busy ([bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/hooks/use-read-failure.ts](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/hooks/use-read-failure.ts)).
- A refused credential save shows an announced notice in its form; a refused removal shows the `credentials-write-failed` toast ([settings-credentials.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/settings/src/client/components/organisms/settings-credentials.tsx)).

### Workspace

- The workspace error notice, `WorkspaceErrorNotice` ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/workspace-error-notice.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/workspace-error-notice.tsx)), an announced `block` notice, shows a workspace failure: in the Project sidebar the `workspace` bundle draws in `shell.sidebar` ([project-sidebar.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx)), and in the Dashboard it draws in `shell.page` ([dashboard.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard.tsx)).
- A failed Projects or Threads load offers Retry, which loads it again; a failed create, rename, color change, delete or copy of a Thread id (`Could not copy`, [use-workspace.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/hooks/use-workspace.ts)) offers none. A lost live stream of the selected Project shows `Live updates stopped` ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/stores/workspace.ts](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/stores/workspace.ts)). Its fallback code follows the retry target: `projects_load_failed`, `threads_load_failed`, else `workspace_action_failed`.
- The Dashboard's Projects list draws its own read failure in place of the list: an announced `block` notice with fallback `projects_load_failed` that keeps its last failure on screen, with Retry busy, while it reads again ([dashboard.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/organisms/dashboard.tsx)).
- The inline name editor shows its validation reason under the name and no toast ([bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/editable-name.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/workspace/src/client/components/molecules/editable-name.tsx)).

### Quarantined bundles and elements that fail

- The `layout` draws one quarantine notice, `BundleHealthNotice` ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/molecules/bundle-health-notice.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/molecules/bundle-health-notice.tsx)), in the left sidebar column of the frame on screen, after the `shell.sidebar` items (and, in the conversation form, after the stack panes) and before the `shell.sidebar.footer` items ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/views/workspace-view.tsx), [bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/components/organisms/project-sidebar.tsx); see the [`layout` README](../../bundles/desktop/layout/README.md)).
- It reads the window's bundle catalog through `doric.catalog/v1`, the platform contract ([packages/bundle/src/contracts/platform.ts](../../packages/bundle/src/contracts/platform.ts)) the window's own platform provides ([packages/desktop/src/client/platform.ts](../../packages/desktop/src/client/platform.ts)), once per mount, so a bundle quarantined later shows after a frame switch remounts the sidebar ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/hooks/use-quarantine-notice.ts](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/hooks/use-quarantine-notice.ts)). With no quarantined bundle, or when the catalog cannot be read, it draws nothing.
- The notice is a `block` notice with the `PackageXIcon`, the warning tone, the title `1 extension couldn't start` or `<n> extensions couldn't start`, the explanation `Turned off: <ids>.`, one details line per bundle (`<id>@<version>`: its reasons, each with its contract, or `no reason given`), no Retry and no announcement ([bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/domain/bundle-health.ts](../../bundles/desktop/layout/src/client/domain/bundle-health.ts)).
- `ElementHost` ([packages/desktop/src/element-host.tsx](../../packages/desktop/src/element-host.tsx), the platform module `desktop/ui/element-host`) mounts another bundle's element. When creating it throws, it logs `Doric: the element <tag> could not be created:` and draws an announced `compact` notice titled `View failed to open` (`element_failed`) with the context `Element`.
